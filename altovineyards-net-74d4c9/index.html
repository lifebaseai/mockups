One man’s dream, an Illinois tradition.

Guy Renzaglia came to Southern Illinois and recreated his Italian homeland, starting the first vineyard and winery in the Shawnee Hills. Today we’re a third-generation family business in our fourth decade: Guy’s children and their spouses, his grandchildren and their partners, and many loyal, highly skilled workers.

  1. The first steps

    Guy Renzaglia, Skip Cosgrove and Ted Wickman bought 50 acres in Alto Pass to rebuild Southern Illinois’ grape and wine industry after Prohibition wiped it out. Ten acres of grapes went in.

  2. Paul becomes winemaker

    Guy’s son Paul became the first winemaker, sharing his father’s dream of the region’s first winery.

  3. Sold out in three days

    A small 600 sq. ft. winery released its first 1,500 gallons of Illinois wine. It sold out in 3 days. There were only 6 other wineries in Illinois.

  4. Building an industry

    Guy and Paul founded the Illinois Grape Growers and Vintners Alliance, with Guy its first President. Paul co-founded the Shawnee Hills Wine Trail.

  5. Winemaker of the Year, twice

    Paul was named Illinois Winemaker of the Year two times. The IGGVA’s Industry Achievement Award now carries Guy’s name.

  6. Champaign opens

    Our second tasting room opened in Champaign, near the University of Illinois, where Guy’s daughter, son-in-law Jim and family began selling our wine in Central Illinois.

  7. The third generation

    Paul’s daughter Elissa became marketing manager, and Guy’s grandson Miguel Womac joined the business.

  8. A new tasting room

    Over 5000 square feet, with the Vine Deck overlooking the vineyard, the pond and the beautiful Shawnee Hills.
