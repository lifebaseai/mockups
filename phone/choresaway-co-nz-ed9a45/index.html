    Under the hood: the five calls she makes

    Each step of the booking is a function call with typed parameters, answered by a stand-in for your booking API built from choresaway.co.nz: the same services, rooms, extras and layout prices. Connected for real, the same calls go to your booking API, or to your own agent.

    get_price

    Price a clean. Use once you know the service, how often, and the rooms (or hours), and again whenever any of those, the extras, pets or parking change. Returns each line, the plan discount, the total including GST and the rough hours.

    service, frequency, rooms[type, count, deep, add_ons, note], hours, parking, storeys, pets[cats, dogs, other]

    find_times

    Open arrival times and the cleaners free at each, for a date at the caller's suburb, nearest the time asked for. Times are Auckland time.

    suburb, date, preferred_time, hours, service

    create_booking

    Create the booking with everything the caller chose, once you have the price, an open time, the cleaner, the caller's name, mobile and street address. It holds the time for 15 minutes and returns the booking to read back; it is not confirmed until confirm_booking.

    service, frequency, rooms[type, count, deep, add_ons, note], hours, parking, storeys, pets[cats, dogs, other], date, time, cleaner_id, customer[name, mobile, email], address[street, suburb], access, instructions, parking_instructions

    update_booking

    Change a held booking before it is confirmed. Give the booking_id and only what changed; for any change to rooms or extras, give the full rooms list again. It prices and checks the time again.

    booking_id, service, frequency, rooms[type, count, deep, add_ons, note], hours, parking, storeys, pets[cats, dogs, other], date, time, cleaner_id, customer[name, mobile, email], address[street, suburb], access, instructions, parking_instructions

    confirm_booking

    Confirm a held booking, only after the caller has said the read-back is correct. Returns the booking reference.

    booking_id
