Questions

What people ask before the first call.

Anything else, just ask. That’s what the free consultation is for.

Is the first conversation free?

Yes. Call or email me for a free 20-minute consultation to see whether we might be a good fit, and then we can schedule an appointment.

Can I see you in person?

If you live in South Carolina, yes: appointments are held in my Little River office or virtually. Texas residents meet with me by teletherapy only.

When are appointments?

In the office: Tuesday and Wednesday, 9:00 to 5:00 ET. Virtual sessions: Monday to Thursday, 10:00 to 6:00 CT (11:00 to 7:00 ET), with some availability on weekends.

What can you help with?

Anxiety, trauma and healing from abuse (I’m EMDR-trained), depression, grief and loss, addiction, self-esteem, and relationship problems: couples and marriages, parents and children, social, school and workplace concerns.

Is what I share kept private?

I provide a confidential, compassionate and supportive space for you to share your concerns, without fear of judgment, while maintaining the highest standards for integrity and ethics.

What about rates and insurance?

Ask me during your free consultation, or see Rates & Insurance on vickiepurdy.com.
