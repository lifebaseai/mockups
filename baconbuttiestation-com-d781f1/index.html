The Bacon Buttie family

From a gazebo to Ward St.

We're a family business, and we butcher our own meat. 100% real meat.

  1. 2002

    Mike, the butcher, heard about a new farmers market in Dunedin. Gas hobs, a frying pan and a flimsy gazebo went in the back of the ute, and the bacon buttie made its debut.

  2. Then

    Word spread. People lined up by the droves for their Saturday buttie, so the gazebo was traded in for a customised food truck.

  3. 2012

    Our dream of a fully-fledged Bacon Buttie Station came true.

  4. Today

    The food truck has retired. You can always find us in our store at 12 Ward St.

  • Mike

    The Bacon Buttie man

    Began carving meat under his father's guidance at 17. Inspired by his Dutch heritage, he cures bacon the traditional way at his butcher shop in Halfway Bush. He bones, cures, smokes, slices and packs. His guarantee: 100% quality and 100% meat.

  • Glenda

    Behind the scenes

    Mike's wife and Tia's mum. You'll find her at the butcher shop cleaning, preparing and organising. She prefers the background, but her role is vital to keeping the business running, and the recipes secret.

  • Tia

    In the driver's seat

    Mike and Glenda's daughter. After years in hospitality and overseas, she's back running the Station. Tia and her team take Mike's meat and turn it into something delicious and hearty.

At the Bacon Buttie Station, what you see is what you eat.

Which days are you open?
7 days a week, for bacon butties, breakfasts and bacon-based burgers.

Yes, GF bread is +$2 on your buttie. Brown bread is $1.
