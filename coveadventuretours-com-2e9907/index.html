Your day, stop by stop

The hard part is ours. The beach is yours.

San Josef Bay is rugged and raw, and getting there takes local knowledge. Tap each stop to see what we take care of.

Stop 1

We drive. You look out the window.

Most places we go are on active logging roads. Our van is equipped for all road conditions and made for the rough road into San Josef Bay.

  • No driving the logging roads yourself
  • A safe and timely way to the remote west coast beaches

Stop 2

No cell signal. No worry.

Out here there is no cellular reception, which can mean real risk for some people. That's why we pack the safety gear and know the way.

  • Safety gear packed for you
  • Guides who know the area

Stop 3

Through old growth rainforest.

The trail runs through old growth rainforest in Cape Scott Provincial Park. Your guides keep it fun and casual.

  • An inside take on the area and its history
  • Pack it in, pack it out, always

Stop 4

Out onto the sand.

The trees open onto a vast sandy beach. Centre stage goes to the nature around us.

  • Sea stacks and sea caves
  • Tidal pools to explore
  • Endless spots to unfasten on the sand

About Cove Adventure Tours

Friends who love the North Island, showing it off.

“Sharing the beauty of Vancouver Island North one hike at a time.”

Cove Adventure Tours Ltd. started as an idea among friends who love hiking, exploring the outdoors and, most of all, Northern Vancouver Island. The goal: be ambassadors for our remote corner of the coast, and share it in a safe and dependable way.

Our guides bring a fun, casual atmosphere, knowledge of the local area, and a push to protect our environment.

Pack it in, pack it out. We always carry garbage bags for impromptu beach cleanups.

Good to know

Questions before you go

Something not here? Email or call, and we'll answer.

How hard is the San Josef Bay hike?

It's an easy 1.5 hour hike, round trip, through old growth rainforest to the beach.

Can I just drive there myself?

The roads are active, often rough logging roads with no cellular reception. We drive them every trip in a van equipped for all road conditions, and we pack the safety gear.

When do you run tours?

We're available for tours year round. Contact us for the schedule and to book.

How big are the groups?

Small. You can also book our guides and 10 passenger van privately for your own group.

Can you take us somewhere else?

Yes. We offer custom hiking tours and are happy to take guests wherever they like on the North Island.
