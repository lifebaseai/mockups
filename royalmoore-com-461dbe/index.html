Quick answers

Before you come in.

When are you open?

Sales is open Monday to Saturday, 8:30 AM to 8:00 PM, and Sunday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.

Which brands do you sell new?

Toyota, Subaru, Mazda, Buick and GMC, across our Royal Moore dealerships in Hillsboro. Royal Moore Pre-Owned has used and certified cars of many makes.

Do you have cheaper used cars?

Yes. We keep a range of used cars priced under $15,000. Pick "Used" and "Under $15,000" in the finder above and send it to our pre-owned team.

Can I get financing before I visit?

Yes. Apply online and you can be pre-approved as soon as today. A payment calculator and trade-in tool help you plan.

What is the Lifetime Powertrain Warranty?

Royal Moore is the home of the Lifetime Powertrain Warranty. Ask our sales team which cars it covers: 503-648-3213.
