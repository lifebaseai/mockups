Hi, I'm Eron

Not because I read about it. Because I lived it.

“Here's what I know now: I was already paralyzed before that fall.”

  1. The dream

    I wanted to be a comedy writer.

    A film degree, then work with National Lampoon and The Laugh Factory. Then a few lost years: headhunting, substitute teaching, bartending.

  2. Baseball

    A six-dollar-an-hour internship.

    In Nashville. Then director of marketing for the Stockton Ports.

  3. Building

    That company became Swagbucks.

    A friend started an online rewards company and I moved back to LA to join him. It became a multibillion-dollar business.

  4. Awakening

    Spiritual beings having a human experience.

    I couldn't unsee it. So I left, and started a new company for the evolution of human consciousness.

  5. The fall

    Forty feet, at Burning Man.

    I woke up in an ICU in Reno to a doctor telling me I'd crushed my spinal cord and would never walk again. I was thirty-eight. My children were five and two.

  6. Dissolution

    When you lose everything that identifies you, what's left is you.

    The company closed. My marriage ended. My parents died. Out of years in that surrender came something simple: service is the answer, integration is the path.

  7. Today

    Genuinely, uncompromisingly joyful.

    Blood pressure 112/68. No medications. I help leaders see what's running underneath: the beliefs, patterns and blind spots shaping every decision. Breakdowns into breakthroughs. Messes into messages.
