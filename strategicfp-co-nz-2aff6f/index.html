How we work

Is inflation quietly eroding your wealth?

We help you find investments that stay ahead of rising costs, and our boutique approach means we stay with you for the long haul.

  1. Getting to know you comes first

    Building for the future, needing income now, or after a big change like selling a business or an inheritance: we listen first, then design the strategy.

  2. Research-backed, and quick to act

    Our approach follows real market changes, not calendar dates. From traditional funds to ETFs, we use whatever truly adds value to your wealth.

  3. Watching over your portfolio

    Personal attention and smart technology keep a close eye on your investments around the clock, and we adjust when conditions shift.

  4. Calm when others panic

    We'll never follow the crowd just because everyone else is. Sometimes the best move is staying steady, and we make those decisions with you.

Questions

What people ask us first

How are you paid?

We're 100% fee-based and receive no income from any third party, so we have no conflicts of interest.

What does a first meeting cost?

Nothing. Initial meetings are without fee or obligation, once you've completed our online Personal Financial Map.

What do you invest in?

Smart, diversified investments around the world, from traditional funds to ETFs, always focused on what truly adds value to your wealth.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Michael is licensed by the Financial Markets Authority (FSP2481) and Ewen is a licensed adviser (FSP23143). Strategic Financial Planning Ltd is a registered Financial Service Provider, FSP 26383.

Can I see what's happening with my money?

Any time. Your secure personal login shows your overall performance, individual transactions and the research behind every decision. Local or global, you'll always know where you stand and why.
