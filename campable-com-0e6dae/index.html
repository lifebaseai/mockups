How Campable works

From “where tonight?” to waking up to birdsong.

  1. Find a place

    Search by town or region, or plan a whole road trip. Every listing shows photos, reviews and the house rules.

  2. Book

    Some places confirm straight away. Others ask the host first, and you're only charged when they say yes.

  3. Stay

    Your arrival details come once you're booked, and they work offline for when there's no signal.

Plain answers

What travellers ask us.

Are there booking fees?

Not for guests. The price you see is the price you pay.

When am I charged?

Some places confirm straight away. Others ask the host first, and you're only charged when they say yes.

Can I come in a caravan or a tent?

Yes. Campable is for people travelling New Zealand by campervan, motorhome, caravan or tent. Every listing shows who can stay.

What if there's no signal when I arrive?

Your arrival details come once you're booked, and they work offline.

How do I book?

On the website, or in the Campable app for iPhone and Android.

What if my plans change?

Our Cancellation Policy sets out what happens. Every guest also agrees to our Rules of Etiquette.
