Questions

What parents ask first.

Anything else, ask on your free call.

Is this tutoring?

No. Tutoring works on content. We train the abilities underneath it, like focus, attention, memory and visual processing, so students can learn any subject more effectively.

How do we start?

With a free consultation by video call, about your child’s individual needs. Then you choose the program that works for your family.

Do we have to come to a center?

Not for everything. The Hybrid Program is half at home, with all equipment provided. Memory and homeschool courses are online, and a full Online Program is coming soon.

Who is it for?

Learners of any age and ability: children who struggle, high achievers working too hard, homeschool families, and adults.

Do the results last?

These skills are brain enhancements that improve learning throughout a lifetime. The benefits don’t just go away when you finish the program.
