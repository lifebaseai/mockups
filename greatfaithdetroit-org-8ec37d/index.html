Our story

A legacy of faith since 1973.

“To teach the Word of GOD, demonstrate the Power of GOD's Love, equip the believers and Impact the world!”Our mission
  1. 1973

    Founded January 7 as Faith Apostolic Temple by the late Elder Royal Titus Bozeman and Corrine Yvonne Bozeman, Dr. Beverly Jackson's parents.

  2. 1987

    A new season as Greater Faith Ministries under Wayne T. and Dr. Beverly Y. Jackson, entrusted with the mantle in 1986.

  3. 1992

    From Inkster to Detroit, for greater influence in the city, the nation and the world.

  4. 2000s

    Impact Network launches, now reaching more than 90 million homes across the United States, the Caribbean and parts of Africa.

  5. 2024

    Impact Great Faith Global brings live worship, teaching, prayer and fellowship to believers anywhere.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What day is service?

Saturday. Sabbath Service, our main weekly worship gathering, is every Saturday at 12:00 PM ET. On Tuesdays there's Noonday Service at 12:00 PM ET and Empowerment Tuesdays online at 7:00 PM ET.

Where are you?

10735 Grand River Ave, Detroit, MI 48204. Get directions.

What should I wear?

Come as you are. You're always welcome here.

Can I worship online?

Yes. Join us for live worship on YouTube. Empowerment Tuesdays, our prayer and Bible study, is online only.

Is Trunk or Treat free?

Yes. It's free, for all ages, and in person, on Saturday, October 31, right after the 12 PM Sabbath Service.

How do I send a prayer request?

Use the prayer form on this page. Our intercessory prayer team prays over every request that comes in.

How can I give?

Give online at greatfaithdetroit.org to support the ministry.
