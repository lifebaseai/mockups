Questions

Straight answers.

Can you pick up from any restaurant?

Yes, any restaurant in our coverage area, including ones that don’t offer their own delivery.

Is it on-demand?

No. Every delivery is scheduled in advance, so the restaurant knows when the driver is coming and you know when the food arrives.

Do you take a commission?

Never. We’re a delivery and logistics provider, not a marketplace. You pay a delivery fee, not a percentage of your sales.

Whose brand do my customers see?

Yours. Deliveries are white-label, and the driver represents your restaurant at the handoff.

Can I see where my order is?

Yes. Follow the status from pickup through completion where live status is available, and get confirmation once it’s delivered.

Will the driver help set up?

For catering, we can help with basic setup and setting out the order.

Can I pass the delivery cost on?

Yes. Our flat-rate fee lets you pass part or all of it to your customers.

We’re a restaurant group. Can you scale?

Our Enterprise programs include negotiated pricing, consolidated billing and high-volume and large-event catering delivery.
