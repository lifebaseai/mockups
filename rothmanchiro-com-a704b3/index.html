Before your first visit

Questions patients ask

Will I see Dr. Rothman herself?

Yes. She works with each patient one on one, and her total attention is on you throughout your entire session, each and every time.

What can she help with?

Back pain, neck pain, headaches, muscular tightness and tension, hip pain, sciatica and frozen shoulder. She can also help you reach your everyday wellness goals.

Can I come in while I’m pregnant?

Yes. If you’re suffering from sciatica or back pain during pregnancy, she can help. Lactation support is offered too.

Do you see children?

Yes. Pediatric care helps with many childhood conditions, including colic, ear infections, headaches, bed wetting and posture. There’s a separate pediatric intake form.

What if traditional medicine hasn’t helped?

Functional medicine may be the answer. As a certified functional medicine practitioner, Dr. Rothman looks for root causes rather than just symptoms, and builds a treatment plan tailored to you.

Is it only adjustments?

No. Services like ShockWave, Redcord, Fascial Stretch Therapy, Kinesio Taping and private yoga reinforce and extend the benefits of chiropractic care.
