Meet the beekeeper

Meet Barna, founder of Tiaki Bees

Barna got his first beehive a few years ago, and he was hooked.

Since then he has finished a New Zealand qualification in Apiculture, read anything and everything he could find about bees, and grown his hives every year. Tiaki Bees began when more and more friends asked to host a hive, and everyone wanted raw, unfiltered honey made in their own backyard.

It exists to help the bees, give back to the environment and encourage healthy, mindful living.

Qualified Apiculturist, DECA Heard on Radio Wanaka
