About Annie

Hello, I'm Annie

I was born in Pakistan, of mixed Ethiopian and Irish heritage, and grew up between Pakistan, Greece and the UK. Travel, nature, music, dance, indigenous healing traditions and ceremony still inspire how I teach.

I believe yoga goes far beyond the mat. It shapes how we live, relate, heal and move through the world.

  1. 2003Thai oil massage training in Chiang Mai, Thailand. Travelling Southeast Asia and Australia, I discover yoga.
  2. 2006I move to the South of France. Ten years there, raising my son and building my yoga career.
  3. 2007My first teacher training, 200 hours with Yoga Arts in India.
  4. 2016Back in the UK, I found Annie's Soul Flow Yoga.
  5. TodaySenior Yoga Teacher, Yoga Alliance UK, 4000+ hours. I teach across Bath, Somerset and Wiltshire, with retreats in Europe.
Questions

Good to know

Where do you teach?

Classes run in Bath and Westbury, and I work across Bath, Somerset and Wiltshire. Retreats take place in the South of France, Greece, Spain and the UK.

What style of yoga is it?

It's a fusion of dynamic Vinyasa flow, grounding Yin, deeply restorative practice, breathwork and accessible meditation. I also teach Hatha, Yoga Nidra and gong and sound baths.

Is yoga therapy only one-to-one?

No. I work with individuals and with groups, meeting each person where they are in their own life journey.

What is Moon Flow?

It's my signature offering for women's hormonal wellbeing. It follows cycles and life stages, lunar connection and natural rhythms, and supports emotional regulation and nervous system balance.

How experienced are you?

I've taught for over 19 years. I hold an advanced Senior Yoga Teacher qualification with Yoga Alliance UK (4000+ hours) and did my first training in India in 2007.
