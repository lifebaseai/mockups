Ngā pātai / Questions

What people ask us

What does the 8-week programme cover?

Everything in the recruitment process: psychometric tests, PAT conditioning, mindset and decision making, and interview preparation. Support carries on after the 8 weeks.

I'm starting from zero. Is that okay?

Yes. Whether you're just starting out or fine-tuning your application, there's a programme for where you are right now. One of our students started with no knowledge of the psychometric testing process.

What's in the PAT?

The NZ Police Physical Appraisal Test is a 2.4km run, press-ups, a vertical jump and a grip test. PAT preparation is included in the 8-week programme.

Can I prepare online?

Yes. The online psychometrics course comes with an e-learning platform: verbal, numerical and abstract reasoning with timed practice tests. Your account is active for 8 weeks from activation.

Who teaches it?

Our lead instructors, Prashant Naikar and Zion Leaupepe. Zion is an ex-NZ Police officer. Her 0:38 overview video on our site breaks down exactly what the programme covers.

Where are you?

The 8-week programme is delivered in Auckland, at 3/46 Broadway, Papakura. We also list Christchurch (Somerfield), Wellington (72 Kemp Street, Kilbirnie) and Hamilton (46B Boundary Road, Claudelands). Ask us what's on near you.
