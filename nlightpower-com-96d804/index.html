Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Call us or email, we're here day and night.

Do I have to pay upfront?

No. Through our partnership program, qualifying property owners can go solar with zero out-of-pocket cost, with payments lower than their current average electric bill.

Which package do I need?

It depends on your monthly use. nSolarLITE covers 550 kWh a month, nSolarBOOST 1,240 kWh and nSolarMAX 1,650 kWh. Try the package finder above, or ask for a free quotation.

Can you build a system just for my needs?

Yes. Besides our basic packages, we deliver tailor-made packages based on your energy consumption.

What if I need help after installation?

Our team is available 24/7 to assist you.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes, we offer commercial and industrial solar as well as residential.

Can I buy parts?

Yes, from our online store, with warehouse pickup.
