What happens at the free water test?

We come to your house and test your water. It takes about 20 minutes, and you see the results on the spot. Then we recommend a system built around your home. It's free and there's no obligation.

How much does a system cost?

The 3 Stage Mounted starts at $2,400, Carbon + Anti-Scale at $2,800 and Carbon + Softener at $3,000. Your final installed price depends on plumbing, pipe size, drain access, electrical needs and system configuration.

Soft water or scale control?

A softener uses softening resin and salt to remove hardness minerals, for genuinely soft water. Anti-scale media controls scale without salt. Your free water test confirms which approach fits your water, plumbing and household.

Do I need a drain or power?

Salt systems need access to a sewer drain. Powered salt-free systems rinse themselves to dirt, landscaping or a storm drain and need one 120V outlet. Electric-free and wall-mounted systems need no drain and no power. Electric-free systems need you to open a manual flush once a month.

Can it match my house?

Yes. Choose polished stainless chrome jackets, tanks custom painted to match your home's exterior, or a system buried and hidden in grade or landscaping where your property allows.

Where do you install?

Homes across Los Angeles, Ventura, Orange and Riverside Counties.
