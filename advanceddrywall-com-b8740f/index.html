Before you call

What people ask before they book

Something else? Ask Daniel directly: (858) 761-1129.

Will I be able to see the patch afterwards?

That's what we work against. Texture is mixed and tested on scrap, held against your wall in your light, then applied. The job ends with a walk-through under a work light, and it isn't done until the repair stops being findable.

What's this going to cost me?

We price it in person, in writing, with the finish level stated. Masking, clean-up, three coats and primer are all in that number. No obligation.

How bad is the dust going to be?

Floors and doorways go in plastic, and adjacent rooms are sealed on bigger jobs. We clean up at the end of every day, not the end of the job.

Why does a one-day repair take three days?

Three coats, and each one dries fully before the next. A one-day quote on a three-coat repair is a quote to skip a coat.

Are you licensed, and how soon can you start?

Licensed and insured, CSLB #761040, BBB A+. We answer 24/7. Call and we'll give you an honest date, then hold it.
