What we do

Everything under one license

From the first steel stud to the last coat, for homes, tenant improvements and commercial shells.

  1. Skim coating & Level 5

    Whole-surface skim coats and Level 4/5 finishes for walls under raking light, wide glass or a high-sheen paint.

  2. Remodels & tenant improvements

    Occupied buildings, phased programmes and out-of-hours work. Selective demo, reframe, board and finish, then off the floor.

  3. Commercial metal stud framing

    Light-gauge steel partitions, soffits, shaft walls and ceiling grid, framed to the drawings and coordinated with your other trades.

  4. Drywall installation

    New build, additions, ADUs and commercial shells hung, taped and finished to the level your spec calls for.

  5. Residential drywall

    Whole-house remodels, additions, garage conversions and single rooms, run to the same standard as the commercial work.

  6. Repairs & patching

    Holes, cracks, water damage and access patches cut back to solid board, rebuilt, and textured until the repair stops being findable.

  7. Insulation

    Thermal and acoustic batts set in the cavity before board goes on, so one crew closes the wall rather than three.

  8. Soundproofing

    Party walls, bedrooms and shared floors: resilient channel, damped board and insulation detailed as a system.

Our promise

Checked under a work light

Every job ends with a walk-through under the harshest light the wall will ever face. If a seam shows or the texture reads wrong, it hasn’t passed, and we keep working until it does.

It costs us an extra hour. It’s the difference between a repair and a patch.

All of this is in the price

  • 01Scope read before a price

    Drawings read or the space walked first. If it won’t build as drawn, we say so before you award it.

  • 02Finish level in writing

    Level 4 or Level 5, stated. Most quotes leave it vague, which makes them impossible to compare.

  • 03Masking & dust containment

    Floors and doorways in plastic, adjacent rooms sealed on bigger jobs.

  • 04Three coats, properly dried

    Each coat dries fully before the next goes on.

  • 05Texture sampled, not guessed

    Mixed and tested on scrap, held against your wall in your light, then applied.

  • 06Primed ready for paint

    A painted-ready surface, and an honest word on whether spot-painting will show.

  • 07Daily clean-up

    Cleaned every day, not at the end of the job. Offcuts and buckets leave with our crew.

  • 08Walk-through together

    Inspected with you under a work light before we call it finished.

Before you call

What people ask first

Straight answers to what we hear most from San Diego homeowners and contractors.

Will I be able to see the patch?

That’s the whole point of how we work. Texture is mixed and tested on scrap, held against your wall in your light, then applied. Then we check it together under a work light.

What’s this going to cost me?

We read the drawings or walk the space first, then give you a written estimate with the finish level stated. In person, no obligation.

How bad is the dust going to be?

Floors and doorways go in plastic, and adjacent rooms are sealed on bigger jobs. We clean up at the end of every day and haul the debris.

Why does a one-day repair take three days?

Three coats, and each one dries fully before the next. A one-day quote on a three-coat repair is a quote to skip a coat.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Licensed and insured, CSLB #761040, BBB A+. Look it up, and ask every other bidder for theirs.
