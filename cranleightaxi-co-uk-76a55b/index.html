Questions

Good to know before you ring.

Ask Cathy: 01483 893 130
How do I book?

Phone 01483 893 130. Book for today, tomorrow or next week. Pre-booking is advisable to avoid disappointment.

What if my flight lands late?

Flight monitoring is free. Your driver arrives 20 to 30 minutes after you land, unless you ask otherwise.

When does the night rate start?

Day rates change to night rates between 11pm and 7am. The meter is set by Waverley Borough Council.

Can you ring me before an early pick-up?

Yes. On request, we'll call you 30 minutes before pick-up for early morning appointments.

Can I ask for a lady driver?

Yes, a lady driver is available on request.

How can I pay?

By credit or debit card through our website, or cash on the day. Business accounts are welcome.

Which areas do you cover?

Cranleigh and the Surrey Hills, with metered taxis based in Shamley Green and Shere. We'll also take you to London, Portsmouth, Southampton or wherever you'd like to go.

Anything I should know for the ride?

Seat belts must be worn, and there's no smoking or eating in the car. The driver takes the most suitable and safest route, unless you ask otherwise.
