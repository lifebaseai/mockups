The founder

Built by someone who needed it

A place to do your best work, built by someone who needed it.

Vincent Cullane, cabinet maker and entrepreneur

Vincent ran his own design and bespoke furniture companies for eighteen years. He knew exactly what a proper workshop needed, and he couldn’t rent one anywhere.

So he built one: two 1911 factory buildings on the Limehouse Cut, converted himself, with the brick, the timber floors and the light kept. Hone opened there in 2018, Bristol followed in 2023 and Tottenham in 2026: 30,000 square feet across two cities.

Your questions answered

Plain answers.

If yours isn’t here, ask on the tour.

What does it cost?
Makerspace from £125 a month. Jewellery from £190. Woodwork from £220 in Bristol, and from £877 in Limehouse plus waste and sundries. Studios in Limehouse £645 a month, all in. Tottenham studio workshops from £210, all in.
Which space is right for me?
Pick what you make and where at the top of the page and you’ll see the space, the price and what’s free now. Or come on a tour and see them all.
Do I need to be a professional?
No. Hone is for people who make things, design things and build companies, and people on their way there.
How do I see it?
The weekly tour in Limehouse, Tuesdays 3.30pm and Thursdays 11.30am, with Jai. About twenty minutes, longer if you get talking. Book it below.
Can I use more than one workshop?
Yes. Members don’t just rent a bench in one: they get into the others too, and more opens up as they move up.
Do people stay long?
Most stay for years, and some have been here more than five.
What are the hours?
The jewellery workshop has 24-hour access. For the other spaces, ask on the tour.
How do the contracts work?
The makerspace is rolling monthly to start. Woodwork benches are private and full time.
Is the induction included?
Yes, the general induction is included with the makerspace.
Where are you?
16 Pixley Street, Limehouse, London E14 7DF, seven minutes from Westferry DLR. Studio workshops in Tottenham, N15, and a cabinet shop in Fishponds, Bristol.
