Why us

We check behind the drywall, not just the stain.

  1. Most of our water calls start with a plumbing failure

    Burst pipes, leaking ice maker lines and overflowing washing machines. That water follows the framing to the floor below, so we check the whole path before we plan the drying.

  2. The first 24 to 48 hours count

    The EPA advises drying water-damaged areas within 24 to 48 hours to prevent mold growth. That is why someone answers our line at any hour, weekends and holidays included.

  3. We dry it, then we rebuild it

    The framing, subfloor and insulation decide how big the job really is. Because we rebuild as well as dry, we check those first, then tell you what can be dried in place and what has to come out.

  4. Certified for water damage and drying

    We're an IICRC Certified Firm, certified for water damage restoration and structural drying.

    Does homeowners insurance cover water damage?+

    It often covers water damage that is sudden and accidental, such as a burst pipe or a failed supply line. Damage from a slow leak over months, or water that came in from outside, is often treated differently. Your policy and your adjuster decide, and only you, as the policyholder, can file the claim.
