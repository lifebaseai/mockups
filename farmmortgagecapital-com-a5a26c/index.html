Questions

Plain answers.

Anything else, call (913) 549-3776.

How much can I borrow?

Loans run from $400K to $50M. Most programs lend up to 70% of the property's value, and certain qualified borrowers may qualify for up to 80%.

Do you lend in my state?

Yes. We finance farm and agricultural properties in all 50 states.

What kinds of property do you finance?

Farms, ranches, dairies, orchards, vineyards, groves, citrus, timberland, hobby farms, country estates, recreation properties, large equestrian operations and other specialty agricultural operations.

Can I borrow against livestock, crops or equipment?

No. Our loans are on real estate only, not on livestock, crops or equipment.

Is there a fixed rate, and a prepayment penalty?

30-year fixed-rate options are available. Most of our programs offer up to 30 years amortization with no prepayment penalties.

I farm part-time. Do I qualify?

Farm loans are available as full-time or part-time farm loans. Which one fits depends on how you run your farm.

What do equestrian properties need?

Equestrian facility loans currently go up to 70% loan to value, for properties of at least 5 acres.
