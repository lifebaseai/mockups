What I help with

Assessment and therapy for:

I specialise in complex trauma, and in relationship difficulties and problems managing emotions, including anxiety and depression.

  • Depression, low mood
  • Repetitive negative thinking (rumination)
  • Relationship difficulties
  • Emotion dysregulation
  • Self-harm
  • Impulsivity
  • Personality disorder
  • Complex, childhood trauma
  • Excessive worrying and overthinking (generalised anxiety)
  • Panic and panic attacks
  • Social anxiety
  • Obsessive Compulsive Disorder (OCD)
  • Health anxiety
  • Phobias
  • Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)

How I work

One therapy, built from several.

After your initial consultation, I'll suggest which approaches I believe might help and why. In treatment I combine parts of different models to suit your experiences, preferences and goals.

  • STSchema TherapySpecial interest
  • EMDREye Movement Desensitisation and ReprocessingSpecial interest
  • DBTDialectical Behaviour TherapySpecial interest
  • CBTCognitive Behavioural Therapy
  • ACTAcceptance and Commitment Therapy
  • CFTCompassion-Focussed Therapy

I typically offer mid- to long-term treatment, unless you ask for a short-term intervention.

Questions

Before you get in touch

How do I start?

With an initial consultation. It's 50 minutes, and I'll learn about you, what brought you to therapy and your goals. Then I'll outline my recommendations.

What does it cost?

£225 for the initial consultation and £225 for each ongoing session. Both are 50 minutes.

Can I use my health insurance?

I'm a registered healthcare provider for Aetna Global Benefits, Bupa International, Cigna and WPA.

Where do we meet?

At 15 Harley Street, Marylebone (Oxford Circus & Bond Street), at 2 Eaton Gate, Belgravia (Sloane Square), or online using Zoom.

Which therapy will we use?

After the initial consultation I'll suggest approaches I believe might help, and why. I'm trained in CBT, ACT, DBT, CFT, EMDR and Schema Therapy, and I usually combine elements of them.

How long does therapy last?

I typically offer mid- to long-term treatment, unless you ask for a short-term intervention.
