Good to know

Questions, answered

Anything else? Call +673 895 3222.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Delivery is free on orders over BND 100.

Can I try equipment before I buy?

Yes. Come to the showroom at The Curve, Gadong, to see and test supplements, apparel and the latest strength and cardio equipment in person. We’re open daily from 10:00 to 19:00 and closed for Friday prayer from 12:00 to 14:00.

Do you design and fit out commercial gyms in Brunei?

Yes, from a home gym corner to a full commercial floor. We handle on-site assessment, the layout and equipment plan, flooring and power planning, delivery and installation, and servicing afterwards.

Which equipment brands do you supply?

We are Brunei’s exclusive distributor for Star Trac, Nautilus, Schwinn, StairMaster, Metcon, Shua Fitness, STEPR, Wattbike, Assault Fitness and Concept2.

Do you service equipment that wasn’t purchased from Stronger?

Yes. We service and repair the gear you bought from us and most other major brands, both cardio and strength.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the space and the equipment. Book a free site visit and we’ll walk you through layout, lead times and pricing before you commit.

Do you offer maintenance contracts?

Yes, for facilities. They cover scheduled preventive servicing plus cable, belt and part replacement, so your equipment keeps performing and your warranty stays intact.
