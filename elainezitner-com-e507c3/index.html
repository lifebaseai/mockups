Questions

Good to know.

Straight answers before we talk.

Who do you coach?

Purpose-driven leaders and dreamers. People who are tired of feeling frustrated and unsatisfied, and ready for more joy, ease, purpose, creativity, connection and fun.

Do I need to know what I want first?

No. You're the expert in you, I'm the expert in change and transformation. If you can't shake the feeling there's something else out there for you, that's a fine place to start.

Big change or small tweak?

Either. A huge life transformation, or a meaningful tweak that brings more joy and vibrancy to the life you have now.

What are your qualifications?

I'm a PCC ICF Certified Coach with a Bachelor of Commerce and a Master's in Organizational Development from Pepperdine University, plus certifications in IEQ9 Enneagram, Brené Brown's Dare to Lead, PROSCI Change Management, Lean Black Belt and YTT Yoga.

How long are the workshops?

Say It Sooner and Who's Leading Who are each 90-minute interactive workshops.

Can I start for free?

Yes. Try the free resource library, or join my newsletter, The Ripple, for tips and stories on leading yourself and others.
