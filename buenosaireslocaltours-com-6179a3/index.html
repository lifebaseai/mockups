Why BuenosTours

Don’t just see Buenos Aires. Understand it.

The city is beautiful, layered, chaotic, elegant, political, nostalgic and alive. A sightseeing tour shows you buildings. A great first day helps it all make sense, so the rest of your trip is easier, richer and more fun.

  1. Feel oriented, not overwhelmed

    Learn how the city fits together, what to watch for and how to spend the rest of your time well.

  2. Ask anything

    Small groups make room for real conversation. Your guide follows your interests, not a memorized script.

  3. No planning on your side

    We handle the route, timing, cemetery admission and public transport. You just enjoy the day.

FAQ

Questions before you book?

Short answers. For anything else, write to us below.

Which option should I choose?
Most first-time visitors choose Full Day Essentials for the best balance of depth, comfort, value and real Buenos Aires context. Choose Half Day for a tight schedule, or add La Boca if it’s on your must-see list.
How much walking is there?
The pace is conversational, with regular stops and breaks. Half Day is the lighter option, and if guide availability allows you can upgrade before the tour or on the day to continue.
Where do we meet?
In Recoleta, near the corner of Av. Santa Fe and Av. Callao, at 10am. Exact meeting details are sent in your booking confirmation email.
Do you run on Sundays?
Yes, Full Day Essentials only. It runs in reverse, starting in San Telmo at 10am and finishing in Recoleta at 5pm.
Is lunch included?
On both Full Day options, always: a local main and a drink. Not on the Half Day.
Can you get us to the start?
Yes, as an optional add-on for the start or end, booked at least 50 hours before your tour.
What if I’m not happy?
Tell your guide at the end of the tour and you receive a full refund.
