About us

We're Chris and Debby, and we Rethink Typical™

We're educators, and a neurodiverse couple. After navigating Chris' late diagnosis, we set out to make autism education engaging and approachable.

We had already transformed traditional teaching in Taiwan by making learning engaging and fun, and saw how the right approach could spark understanding and joy. Auticate is that approach, for autism.

  • We believe the world is better with autistic people in it.
  • Our goal: to see every autistic person become their most accepting and able self.
  • We've faced the challenges and confusion, and we share what we've learned.

autism+educate=Auticate

Say it AUT-i-cate.

Why the elephant?

Wisdom, patience and a remarkable memory: the strength to navigate life's challenges. Elephants also stand for deep family bonds and loyalty, like the steady, supportive community we're building.

Questions, answered plainly.

Do I need a diagnosis?

No. Whether you're diagnosed, self-identified, or still exploring, you're welcome here.

Is any of it free?

Yes. Our videos are free on YouTube, and so are The Autistic Roadmap guide and the four-part mini-course, Is This Autistic Burnout?

I'm not autistic. Is this for me?

Yes. If someone you care about is autistic, we'll help you understand and genuinely connect with them.

Who makes it?

We do: Chris and Debby, educators and a neurodiverse couple. Chris was diagnosed late, and everything we make is rooted in lived experience.

Where else can I find you?

Our videos are on YouTube, and we're on Instagram and Facebook too.
