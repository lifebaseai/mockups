Yes. Sarnamg has served clients in the U.S.A., U.K., Canada, the European Union, Australia, the Middle East, South East Asia and many other countries since 2003. You book online.

What do you need to prepare my kundli?

Your date of birth, place of birth and time of birth.

How is Vedic astrology different?

Vedic astrology puts your Moon sign first. Western astrology puts your Sun sign first.

What can I ask an astrologer about?
