How much does it cost?

A 45-minute meeting with Dr. Kurt is $260, and 90 minutes is $520. It's the same fee in person, by video or by phone, and we don't charge extra for couples or families.

Our fees are on the high end. Our expertise is very specialized, and because of our approach we get great results. Free articles and quizzes and low-fee digital products (under $50) keep our help within everyone's reach.

Does insurance pay for counseling?

Most health insurance will reimburse counseling, within policy limits. We don't take insurance or bill insurers, to protect your privacy. If you'd like to claim, check whether your plan reimburses out-of-network mental health counseling. Many plans have reimbursed Guy Stuff patients.

Why we work this way: billing insurance means a mental disorder diagnosis on your medical record, treatment reports to your insurer, and the insurer deciding what you work on and for how long. We believe that should be your choice.

What's men's counseling like?

It's like talking to a buddy in your living room. Only this buddy really knows what he's talking about, and gives you advice that's been proven to work. We're your partner and resource the whole way through.
