Questions

What the diaspora asks us first

How do I avoid construction scams in Africa?

Work with professionals who passed our document check, see daily photo and video proof, and let independent inspectors validate each stage. Your funds are held and only released when you approve.

Can I use my own contractor?

Yes. Invite your own contractor to manage the project on DiasporaBuild, or browse our verified marketplace.

How are payments secured?

By milestone. Funds are held securely and released on your approval, so the contractor is paid stage by stage, once the work is validated.

Is DiasporaBuild free to use?

You can get started free. During our launch offer there is 0% marketplace commission on your project, and advanced options remain available on subscription.

Which countries are supported?

Cameroon, Senegal, Ivory Coast, Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya and across Africa.

What happens if there is a dispute with my contractor?

A fair mediation process protects all parties, backed by a timestamped record of every action on the project.
