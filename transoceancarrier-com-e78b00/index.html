FAQ

Straight answers.

To the questions shippers actually ask. No jargon, no runaround.

What is Precision Drayage?

Our operating philosophy, refined over 35+ years at the Ports of Long Beach and Los Angeles: trusted relationships with steamship lines, terminals and equipment partners, paired with disciplined planning and execution. The result is containers moved safely and efficiently, port to door or rail, with zero demurrage and zero detention.

How do you handle port and terminal congestion?

We're on-site at every terminal we touch. When gates open, slots clear, or chassis run short, we're already moving. Congestion, chassis shortages and customs holds get handled before they're your problem.

Why is drayage from LA and Long Beach different?

The Port Mile is the hardest mile. Miss the first-mile window, and delays ripple through everything. We stop the dominoes before they fall.

Why do shippers choose TOC?
