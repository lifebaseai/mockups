About us

We live, breathe & eat bikes.

(Well, maybe not eat.)

Would you take cooking advice from someone who only knew how to eat? At Westwood Cycle, experience is the difference. Some of us ride over 7,000 miles a year and still hold full-time jobs, and that commitment goes into every bike we sell and fix.

Mark, Dean, Davis, Jerry and Wendy: more than 100 years of bicycle sales and service between us. We ride nearly every day, on the road or on the trails.

Same location in Westwood since the 1940s. Same family since the 1960s. Thank you for telling your families and friends about us.

Good to know

Quick answers.

How do I book a repair?

Call us at (201) 664-1688 or send a request below. We start every repair with a no-obligation estimate of the work that’s needed.

Can I test ride before I buy?

Yes. Visit us for a test ride, and we’ll make sure you’re on the right bike and the right frame size. For demo bikes, give us a call.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10am to 6pm. Closed Sunday.

Can I shop after hours?

Yes. Call us to make an appointment to shop after hours.

Do you take trade-ins?

Yes, we have trade-in programs for both bikes and e-bikes.
