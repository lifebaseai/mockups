Questions

What buyers ask first

What is your minimum order quantity?

It depends on how you buy. Sellers and planners can order from 1 carton through our US warehouse. For custom colors, branding and packaging, it depends on the product and order quantity, and we review your requirements before quoting.

Can I order samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Ask for a sample kit using the form below.

How do you ensure fast delivery for bulk orders?

Wholesale orders ship with mixed container loading and DDP service. Selected products are stocked in our US warehouse for 3–5 day US shipping.

Can you share compliance documents?

Yes. Food-contact test reports and applicable compliance documents are available by product and market. Wholesale programs are compliance ready, FDA/LFGB certified for big retail.
