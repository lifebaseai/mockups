Questions

Questions we get

What vehicles do you have?

New Dodge, Chrysler, Jeep and Ram, from the Ram 1500 and Jeep Wrangler to the Chrysler Pacifica and Ram ProMaster, plus certified and used vehicles and work trucks.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Apply online with our secure application and get pre-approved quickly, with flexible options to fit your budget.

Can I trade in my current vehicle?

Yes. Enter your car's details online for an instant offer, then sell it to us or trade it in. Tick "I'm trading in" when you plan your visit and we'll be ready.

How do I book a service appointment?

Schedule service online, or call the service desk at 702-432-2434. Service is open 7am to 6pm on weekdays and 7am to 5pm on Saturday.

Do you have service specials?

Yes. Our current service specials are on our site, along with MOPAR Express Lane service.

What warranties come with my purchase?

Every Certified Pre-Owned vehicle is backed by a factory warranty. Ask us about MOPAR Vehicle Protection too.
