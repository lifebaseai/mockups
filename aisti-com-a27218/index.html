Technology

Foam formingno plastic, no synthetic binders

  1. Three ingredientsWood fibre, water and a foaming agent similar to those in everyday products.
  2. Millions of tiny bubblesThe foam spreads the fibres evenly into a light, porous structure.
  3. Bonded by natureAs the foam dries, the fibres bond through hydrogen bonds. No binders added.
  4. A tile that absorbs soundLightweight, durable and highly effective in sound absorption. A patented material innovation.

Questions

Askedand answered

Does it cost more than a conventional tile?

No. Same technical performance, same competitive price, with a different material approach.

Will it fit our ceiling system?

Yes. Aisti Aalto® tiles fit standard suspended systems.

How does it perform on sound and fire?

Class A sound absorption and B-s1,d0 fire safety. The tiles also use Aisti's patented fire-retardant solution.

What is it made of?

Natural wood fibre, water and a foaming agent. No plastic binders, no mineral wool, no synthetic shortcuts.

Is it good for indoor air?

It meets the French VOC A+ and M1 indoor air classifications, and it is odour-neutral to install.

Where can I buy it?

Through Ogeborg in Sweden, AGS Group AS in Norway and Beyond Rational in Denmark. In Finland and other regions, straight from Aisti.
