      Why it lasts

      Most perfume is mostly not perfume.

      Mass market perfumes hold only 5 to 10% fragrance. Ours are Extraits de Parfum, the most concentrated form recognized by traditional French perfumers, blended at 35 to 50%. The scent is deeper, and it evolves through the day.

      From the founder

      Taught by a perfume chemist. Now made only from what grows.

      Bob Comitz started creating fragrances 45 years ago, after becoming friends with a perfume chemist. He learned the trade with the modern compounds the industry uses.

      Today he makes his fragrances only from pure flower absolute oils and oils drawn from plants and trees.

      Questions, answered.

      What does it cost?

      $25 for 10 ml, down from the regular $30, and $15 for a 5 ml sample. These are introductory prices for a limited time, and shipping is free.

      How do I order?

      Choose your scent and size, then check out. It’s quick and easy.

      Are they for men or women?

      Both. Every one of our fragrances is unisex.

      What are they made from?

      Natural flower extracts and essential oils, with pure flower absolutes and oils from plants and trees.

      What is Extrait de Parfum?

      The most concentrated, luxurious form of fragrance recognized by traditional French perfumers. Ours is blended at 35 to 50%, against 5 to 10% in most perfumes.

      Which scents can I choose?

      Ambergris, Blue Lotus, Jasmine, Musk, Patchouli and Sandalwood.
