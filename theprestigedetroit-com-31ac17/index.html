    Good to know

    Before you come.

    Where are you?

    22925 W 8 Mile Rd, Detroit, MI 48219. Open in Google Maps.

    How do I get a table?

    Pick your night above and send a request, or call us at (313) 766-5573.

    When is brunch?

    Saturday and Sunday, 11 AM to 4 PM. Grits dishes, French toast, waffles, omelettes and a kids menu.

    Is Sunday Supper free?

    Yes. Every Sunday from 4 to 9 PM: great food, all-styles dance music and the NFL on the big screen, with free admission.

    When is karaoke?

    Every Wednesday from 5 PM. Professional sound and lighting, an intimate stage and a grown crowd. No dive-bar vibes here.

    Can I order online?

    Yes. Find Order Online on our menu page.
