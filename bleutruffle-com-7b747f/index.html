The honest answers

The questions he actually gets.

How much does it cost?

Plates start at $16.99 and the minimum order is $15. Catering starts at $1,000, which covers 15 guests, then $70 each. Private chef evenings are quoted per evening: a real number, not a range, before anything is booked.

How far ahead do you need?

Menu orders go in by 6pm and Chef Al confirms the time with you. The cheesecake needs forty eight hours, and so do large cookie orders.

Where do you serve?

Sacramento and the surrounding region, including Elk Grove, Folsom, Granite Bay and El Dorado Hills.

Is the food actually organic?

Yes. Organic is the standard in this kitchen, not an upgrade you pay extra for.

How do you handle allergies?

Allergies and dietary needs are workable. Tell us early and they get built into the menu.

Can you make a specific dish?

A private chef evening starts from a menu built with you, so tell us the dish you have in mind when you write.
