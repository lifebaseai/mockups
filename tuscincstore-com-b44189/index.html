FAQ

Before you buy

The questions we hear most. If yours isn't here, call us at 717-436-5591.

How much does a shed or garage cost?

It depends on size, style and options. Buildings in stock right now run from $5,684 for a 10x16 A-Frame to $10,601 for a 12x24 A-Frame Garage, or from $279 a month on rent-to-own. For anything custom, our site has instant pricing.

How long from order to delivery?

Buildings marked "Ready for delivery" are built and waiting on our lots. For a custom build, call us for timing.

Is delivery included?

Local delivery and placement are included, no hidden fees. Delivery is free within 50 miles of Mifflintown and within 30 miles of Spencerport and Stittville.

What site prep do I need?

A level, accessible spot: gravel pad, clearance and access. We'll walk you through it, and our crew offers full site prep so your building lands ready to use.

Do I need a permit?

Ask us when you call. As one customer put it, "They helped me get all the documents I needed for my permit."
