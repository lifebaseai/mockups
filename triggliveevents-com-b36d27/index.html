Impossibility and possibility

A new breed of magician.

He likes robots more than rabbits. And before magic was his full-time job, he was a technology consultant at Deloitte.

That corporate experience is why Trigg uses magic to connect leadership with employees, communicate a message, and inspire positive change. His tech-infused shows have wowed audiences from London to Las Vegas and on cruise ships around the world.

He caught the magic bug at a friend's fourth birthday party in Australia, emptied the local library of its magic books, and never stopped. Today he lives in Los Angeles.
