FAQ's

Good to know.

Something else? Call or text 716.800.7543.

Can we bring our own drinks?

Yes. Bring your own beer, wine and food. Every boat has coolers to use.

How much does it cost?

From $39 a person for a 2 hour tour. The Tipsy Tiki boat is $300 for a 75 minute private charter for up to 6.

How big a group can you take?

6 to 40 people on one boat. For larger groups, add more boats: whether you’re a party of 6 or 92, we’ve got a ride for you.

How long is a cruise?

About 2 hours on Buffaloha, the Party Barge and the Wave Wagon. Tipsy Tiki charters are 75 minutes.

Where do we board? Is there parking?

301 Ohio Street, Buffalo, NY 14204. Parking on-site is free.

Can we play our own music?

Yes. Every boat has a Bluetooth sound system, so load up your playlist.

Do we have to pedal?

Only on our green cycleboat, and only if you want to. It also has a motor, so you can dance instead.

Is there a bathroom?

Buffaloha has a bathroom onboard. On the cycleboat tour we make one stop for restroom use.
