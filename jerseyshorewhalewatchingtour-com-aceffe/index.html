Before you sail

Questions, answered

How long is the whale watching tour?

Tours typically last 3–4 hours, depending on conditions. The dolphin tour is 2 hours.

Is the tour suitable for children?

Yes. Our trips are family-friendly for all ages, with indoor and outdoor viewing.

What if we don’t see whales?

On whale watch tours of 3 hours or more, you get a free return trip on a future sailing. It doesn’t cover dolphin-only or group bookings. After Oct 15, return trips reschedule to spring, and a $20 processing fee applies.

When should we arrive?

30 minutes before departure, at Belmar Marina, 905 NJ-35. Boarding closes shortly before we leave.

Where do we park?

Public marina parking is available at Belmar Marina.

When is the season?

April through December, with weekday deals and special event sailings. Popular dates sell out, so book early.
