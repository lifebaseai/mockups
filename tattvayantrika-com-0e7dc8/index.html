Questions

Before you start.

What does it cost to ask about my project?

Nothing. Send your project details and you get a free review and a quote.

Will my project stay private?

Yes. Consultations are 100% confidential. The ID you share is stored securely, used only to confirm you're a student or professional, and never shared.

What will you need from me?

Your contact details, the project title and description, the help you need and the software involved. You can attach a ZIP, RAR, PDF or images up to 25MB, and we ask for a college or company ID card for verification.

Which software do you work in?

ANSYS Workbench, Mechanical, Fluent, CFX, Discovery and Explicit Dynamics, plus Abaqus, LS-DYNA, OptiStruct, SolidWorks, CATIA and MATLAB.

Do you help with BTech, MTech and PhD research?

Yes. End-to-end guidance on methodology, setup and paper results.

How do the courses run?

Through weekly drip modules, with an extra month of access afterwards for review and mastery.
