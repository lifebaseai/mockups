Mockup notes

For the Hekayet Hadaf team: what changed

Your homepage, kept recognisably yours: your logo, purple and orange, the glowing map, your services, clients and method, all in your own words. What changed is the order, the clarity, and what a visitor can do on the page.

Layout

  1. One calm first screen: one promise, one main button, one call button. The six-times marquee, side social rail and floating buttons are gone. See it
  2. Deraya Forum 3 is now a clear badge on the hero, not a ticker people scroll past.
  3. No empty boxes: every section is visible on first load, even on slow phones.
  4. On phones, a slim bar keeps "call" and "free consultation" one tap away.

Content structure

  1. Proof first: your clients and platforms sit right under the hero. See it
  2. Your work reel stays near the top, with links to your full portfolio and socials. See it
  3. Services become a clean numbered index: all six in your own words, with their parts where your site lists them, with the sectors you serve beside it. See it
  4. Your FAQ now shows answers, using only facts from your site. See it
  5. Vision 2030 and your guides keep their place, shorter and easier to scan; each guide opens its own article on your blog.

User journey

  1. Every button leads to one place: the free consultation. See it
  2. The form becomes "start your story": pick a sector, a goal and a city, and the request writes itself, with your work in that sector and your 3-step method.
  3. The page ends where it starts: the same free consultation, your phone, both offices and email.
