Where you stay in Ireland

Welcome to Broomfield Lodge.

A newly built stone and granite country house in the green rolling hills of County Kildare, just 50 minutes south of Dublin Airport.

The lodge is privately owned and run by the Moore family, who have lived in the Broomfield area for more than 150 years.
