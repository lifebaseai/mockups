FAQ

Before you book

How big are the groups usually?

Usually 6 to 8 riders. We split into smaller groups with a second guide when needed.

Can I join as an enduro beginner?

Yes. Beginner groups and mixed-group splits are supported in our guiding format.

Are tours conducted in English?

Yes, tours are guided in English.

Do I need a visa to come to Georgia?

Many travelers can enter Georgia visa-free for short stays; others use the official e-visa or consulate process. It depends on your nationality and trip purpose, so check current Georgian government guidance before you book flights.
