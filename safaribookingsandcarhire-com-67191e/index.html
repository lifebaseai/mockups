Questions

Good to know before you go.

How does renting a car work?

Choose the vehicle that suits your trip and contact the host with your destination and rental period. Once the host accepts, reserve and pay online to SBACH. On pickup day, meet the host, sign the rental agreement and drive.

Will I pay anything at pickup?

Possibly local extra fees or a refundable security deposit, if applicable. Return the vehicle at the end of your rental period.

I'm travelling solo. Isn't a safari expensive?

It costs more alone, which is why there are carpool rides. Join a one-way or round trip to the national parks, pay only the transport, and book your own hotel or lodge.

Which vehicle should I choose?

A 4x4 for off-road destinations, a 4x4 safari jeep with an open roof top for game drives, a comfortable bus for groups, and a luxury car for a wedding or business occasion.

Can I pay for my safari over time?

Yes. You can finance your safari through flexible SBACH Vacation Loans.
