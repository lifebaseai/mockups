Before you come

Good to know.

Anything else? Call us at 416-892-4785.

Do I need skates?

No skates. It's off-ice. Bring a hockey stick and clean indoor shoes; gloves are recommended.

Are the pucks real?

Yes. We use real pucks on real nets.

Who can play?

All ages and all skill levels: kids, teens and adults. Just show up and play.

How much is a drop-in?

60 minutes is $23, 90 minutes is $31 and 120 minutes is $36, plus HST.

Is there a membership?

Yes. Unlimited off-ice practice all season at one flat price, from $99 a month. Cancel anytime.

Can we book the whole place?

Yes, for a birthday party or team event: private access to every game plus your own party room. We handle setup and cleanup.
