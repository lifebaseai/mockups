About us

Three generations. One shop on the Langley Bypass.

Since 1985, JMB Engraving has passed its craft down through the family. Today Jessica and Kyle, the third generation, own the shop, and Mac manages production.

You may notice we don't carry a ton of products. That's on purpose: every customer is different, so we focus on quality over quantity.
