Questions

Before you book.

How much does it cost?

A Nutrition Consultation is Rs 500, a Nutritional Assessment Rs 600, a Customised Diet Plan Rs 1,500, and the 3 Month Transformation Plan Rs 7,000.

Can I consult online?

Yes. Book a video consultation and join from home. Online booking is open 24 hours.

Which plan is right for me?

For guidelines on one condition, start with a Consultation. To know your exact calorie needs, choose the Assessment. For a full plan for weight loss, weight gain, PCOS, anemia or diabetes, choose the Customised Diet Plan. For 90 days of follow ups and support, the Transformation Plan.

What does the assessment include?

BMI, IBW, BMR, TDEE, a calorie calculation and portion control guidance, so your nutrition advice fits your body and your goal.

When is the clinic open?

Monday to Saturday, 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM. Closed on Sunday.
