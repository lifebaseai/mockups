Questions

Before you call

When can I start flying?

Flying starts in 2026, with a limited number of 50-hour flight blocks for select, experienced regional pilots. ANG launches in Q4 2026 and the full Cirrus Training Center opens in late 2026.

Where are you?

At Tri-Cities Airport (KTRI), in our exclusive FBO office space. Open in Google Maps.

Which aircraft will you fly?

Cirrus. A second SR22T is being acquired now, two factory-new SR20 G7s with Safe-Return™ Autoland arrive in Q4 2026, a Vision Jet joins in 2027, and another SR22T holds a spot in the 2027 production queue.

Can I lease back my aircraft?

Yes. Lease-back puts your aircraft to work in our flight school and Cirrus Part 135 charter operation, with 100% bonus depreciation potential, accelerated over 1 to 7 years.

Do you have hangar space?

Yes: secure private and shared hangar space, with capacity for up to 20 Cirrus aircraft.

When does charter start?

Cirrus 135 charter is coming in 2028.
