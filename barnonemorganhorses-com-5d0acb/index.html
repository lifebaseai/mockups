Our history, by Harvey

A family of horses, one foal at a time.

  1. The first mares

    Shawnee Lass, Ramul’s Princess and Ramul’s Bay Comet

    Shawnee Lass was 17 when we bought her and had many foals for us before retiring at 26. She would have a newborn up and walking within minutes. She was one of the best!

  2. California

    Bombo, the family horse

    We broke him to drive single and double. He could be ridden or driven by child or adult alike. One of the greatest horses we have owned.

  3. 1994 · Back in Wyoming

    S Bar B Pierre Noire

    A four-year-old smoky black stallion, nearly 16 hands. Reading his pedigree, we found he was a grandson of Shawnee Lass, our very first mare. That was the beginning of a great adventure.

  4. Pierre’s first foal

    A palomino colt

    Ramul looked into the corral and said, I never got anything like that! It was just the beginning of buckskin and palomino foals.

  5. August 2009

    Pierre dies at 19

    Pierre would do anything you asked of him. He is greatly missed.

Questions people ask Harvey

Can I come and see the horses?

Yes. Visitors are always welcome. To make the most of your time, reach out to Harvey well in advance so he can plan for you. Plan a visit

Where is the ranch?

355 Bar None Drive, Thermopolis, Wyoming, about 3.5 miles south of town on Highway 20. GPS will lead you right to us.

Do you have gaited Morgans?

Yes, our own line, with Mtn Edge Black Chrome standing and Bar Non Hickory as our junior gaited stallion. See the bloodlines

Can you board or train my horse?

We offer boarding, training and breeding. Ask Harvey about facilities and availability.
