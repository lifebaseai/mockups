Before you head over

Are you open right now?

Mon–Fri 6AM–2PM, Sat–Sun 7AM–2PM at Bella's North. If we're ever closed during normal hours, you'll see a message at the top of the website, so pretty please don't call just to ask.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Order online for pickup or delivery.

How much notice for catering?

48 hours, please. Each platter has 12 servings. Use the planner above to work out your order.

Where's the full menu?

Six signatures up top, but the full menu runs deeper: breakfast sandwiches, lox, schmears, coffee, the works. See the North menu.

Do you have an allergen list?

Yes, it's on our menu page.

When does Bella's South open?

The Bagels & Bistro at Creekwalk is under construction, with its menu coming early 2027. Follow the build on Instagram.
