Questions

The short answers.

Where does Change Water come from?

The Carrizo-Wilcox aquifer, deep in the heart of Texas.

What makes it different from other water?

It is pH balanced with electrolytes, and it is oxygenated. It has become famous for its pristine taste and beautiful bottles.

Why oxygenated?

Oxygenated water tastes better. And when your water tastes better, you drink more.

Where does my money go?

50% of the proceeds are donated directly to the SaulPaul Foundation, which supports nonprofit organizations in the areas where Change Water is sold.

What is the SaulPaul Foundation?

A 501(c)3 funded and supported by the owners, employees and corporate affiliates of Change Water. A launchpad for young people, and a platform for community members to serve as community leaders.

How do I become a ChangeMaker?

Send a note below and pick "ChangeMakers". The program brings together citizens, organizations and corporations to #BeTheChange.
