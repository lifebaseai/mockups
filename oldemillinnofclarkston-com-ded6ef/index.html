Good to know

Questions guests ask.

Anything else? Call (248) 623-0300 or text (248) 778-5068.

How far is Pine Knob?

About five miles. That goes for both Pine Knob Music Theatre and the Pine Knob Ski and Snowboard Resort.

Are the kayaks and pedal boats free?

Yes, for registered guests, subject to season, weather, safety conditions and availability.

Which rooms are right on the lake?

The Premium Queen, the Premium Lakefront Room with Two Full Beds, and the Presidential Honeymoon Suite. The off-water rooms include lake access and kayaks.

Which rooms sleep four?

The Premium Lakefront Room with Two Full Beds and the Presidential Honeymoon Suite.

Can I cook?

Selected rooms have a kitchenette: the Premium Queen, the Two Full Beds room and the Deluxe Queen. The Presidential Honeymoon Suite has a full kitchen.

Can I bring my pet?

Pet-friendly studios are at our sister location, Clarkston North, 6853 Dixie Hwy.

Is there parking?

Yes, parking is available.
