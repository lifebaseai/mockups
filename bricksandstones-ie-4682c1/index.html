Why FlexOre

Every range, the same easy formula.

Developed over 20 years and handcrafted, so no two pieces are ever the same. Whichever range you choose, you get all four of these.

Ultra-lightweight

Thin, light panels that are easy to handle, with none of the heavy lifting of solid brick.

Highly flexible

Panels bend naturally around corners and curved surfaces at room temperature.

DIY-friendly

Cut or shape it by hand, and lay it with or without grout.

Space-saving

A thin veneer, so your walls keep their room and you get the look of stone.

Made for Irish weather.

Made in Hungary, every piece has been tested against rain, frost and heat. It's UV and weather resistant, so the colour holds outdoors too. Every product comes with a 2-year warranty.

Before you start

Good to know.

The questions people ask us most, answered straight.

Can I fit it myself?
Yes. FlexOre is made for DIY-friendly installation. We recommend asking a qualified specialist which adhesive suits your surface.
Can it go outside?
Yes. It's UV and weather resistant. Outdoors, waterproofing is essential.
Bathrooms and other wet areas?
Yes, as long as you seal the finish with a protective lacquer.
Floors or driveways?
No. FlexOre is for walls only.
Will it go around corners?
Yes. Panels bend naturally around corners and curved surfaces at room temperature.
Can I mix ranges?
Yes. Collections combine across the range for a balanced, cohesive finish, grouted or seamless.
