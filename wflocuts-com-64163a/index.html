Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you work with my hair type?

Yes. Flo works with all the curls: tight, super, loose and wavy, for men, women and children. Natural textures are all she does.

Where are you, and do I need an appointment?

Majestic Style Lounge, in collab with Corte Latino Barbershop, at 1700 Decker Blvd, Unit B, Columbia, SC 29206. Visits are by appointment only.

Will I learn to style my curls at home?

Yes. Personalized education is part of the mission: helping you nurture and maintain your crown with confidence, both in the lounge and at home.

Can you color curly hair?

Yes. Flo trained in blonding curls with Forever Curls in 2019 and completed the Forever Curls Color Bootcamp in Miami in 2025.

Why plant-based products?

Plant-based ingredients come from plants, fruits, nuts and seeds. They're packed with vitamins, minerals and antioxidants that nourish the hair and promote scalp health.

#FloCares

Your support lets Flo partner with international, faith-based organizations that help children in physical, social, economic and spiritual need, so they can be released from a life of poverty.
