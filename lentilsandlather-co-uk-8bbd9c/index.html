How a refill shop works

No packaging.
No guesswork.

Many of our products come from dispensers. You bring the vessel, we skip the plastic.

  1. Bring your own

    Jars, tubs and bottles from home. Refill them and reuse them.

  2. Fill up

    Pasta, cereals, grains, herbs and spices. Shower gel, shampoo and cleaning liquids too.

  3. Take only what you need

    No pre-packed quantities, so you keep better control over your spending.

    Good questions

    Before your first refill

    What do I bring?

    Your own containers: jars or tubs for kitchen staples, bottles for shower gel, shampoo and cleaning liquids. You refill and reuse them, so there's no packaging.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes. Order from our online shop and we deliver all over South and Central Manchester and Stockport every Wednesday, by electric cargo bike wherever we can.

    Can I click and collect?

    Yes. Order online and collect from our Withington or Marple shop.

    Is it just food?

    Food is our biggest section, but we also stock a wide range of household cleaning and personal care products.

    Where are your shops?

    One in the Withington and Didsbury area of Manchester, and one in Marple, in Stockport.

    Can you stock something I can't find?

    Because we're independent, we choose what we stock to match what our customers need. Something missing? Let us know.
