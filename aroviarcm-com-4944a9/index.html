The weekly report

Four numbers. Every week. On one page.

Every plan we make is built on them. You always know what moved, and why.

  • Days in AR Lower is better

    total unpaid claims ÷ average daily charges

    How long it takes, on average, to turn a visit into payment.

  • Clean claim rate Higher is better

    claims accepted first time ÷ claims submitted

    How many claims leave your office correct.

  • Denial rate Grouped by reason

    denied claims ÷ claims submitted

    How often payers say no. We group denials by reason to find the cause.

  • Collection rate Higher is better

    payments ÷ (charges − contractual adjustments)

    How much of what you are owed actually arrives.

Straight answers

What practices ask us first.

Anything else, ask us. We reply in hours.

Do you guarantee collections?

No. We never promise guaranteed collections. We promise the work, the tracking and a clear report.

What does the pilot cost?

The 30-day pilot is paid on collections, so if nothing is collected, the fee is $0. Fee models are discussed on the first call; a monthly rate per team member is the other option.

Who do you work with?

Independent practices of one to ten doctors across the United States, and small billing companies.

How do you handle patient data?

A Business Associate Agreement comes before any patient data. We work on company systems only, with a screened, HIPAA-trained team.

Am I locked in after the pilot?

No. It is month-to-month after the pilot.

Do you do hospital billing?

Not yet. Full revenue cycle support for hospitals is planned for the future as we grow.
