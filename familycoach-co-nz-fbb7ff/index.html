Hi, I’m Lisa

From English teacher to family coach

I was born in Samoa and raised in a cross-cultural, blended family in Aotearoa New Zealand. Complex family dynamics shaped my life, and they became the reason I changed course.

For over 30 years I’ve worked as an educator, mediator, coach, mindfulness teacher and guidance counsellor, including with The Peace Foundation, Brainwave Trust and Great Potentials Foundation. I’ve retrained in positive psychology, conscious parenting, mindfulness and modern relational tools.

This is not about fixing you. It’s about gently coming home to yourself, one brave step at a time.

Honest answers

Is my approach right for you?

It’s likely a good fit if you:

  • Are looking for short-term support (between 2–10 sessions)
  • Appreciate a practical, forward-focused approach to your current situation
  • Are motivated to explore evidence-based strategies and tools for connection
  • Are open to working on your wellbeing between sessions
  • Value working with someone experienced, accredited, and grounded in both coaching and mediation

Possibly not if you:

  • Are looking for long-term therapy or support over many months or years
  • Prefer to work only with a psychologist or legal specialist in mediation
  • Want a low-cost option, and qualifications matter less to you
