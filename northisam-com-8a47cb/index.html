Questions

Good to know.

Is there a minimum order?

No. We print one copy at a time, from a single keepsake to a small run for family and friends.

Do I have to print at the end of the year?

No. Pin your first memory in minutes and print whenever you’re ready. There’s no commitment.

Can I order more copies later?

Yes. Order extra copies whenever you want. We keep your files safe.

Who can see my memory room?

Only you. Your room is private by default, and only you hold the key.

What covers and paper can I choose?

Hardcover, softcover and cloth, with foil options. Photo books come on premium matte or gloss paper, and notebooks come blank or lined.

Where are you?

We’re a local print shop in Bahria Town, Rawalpindi. Your book is printed and bound right here.
