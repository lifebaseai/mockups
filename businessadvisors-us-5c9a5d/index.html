How much is my business worth?

We provide a Broker's Opinion of Value to help you understand what your business is worth now, and what it might be worth in the future. The gap between the two is your Value Gap, and closing it is how you become ExitReady.

Will my employees or customers find out I'm selling?

Your sale remains private until you're ready to announce it, and we market your business to qualified buyers while protecting your identity.

I'm not ready to sell yet. Can you still help?

Yes. Our exit planning lets you start with an initial engagement and add levels as you go: the triggering event, value enhancement, then the decision. Or try our free Value Gap Discovery app, which takes about 15 minutes.
