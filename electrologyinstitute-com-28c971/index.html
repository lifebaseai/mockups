Questions

What students ask first.

Can I study online?

Yes. We offer hybrid learning for all programs: a combination of weekly onsite hours and online hours.

I don't live in Massachusetts. Can I still train with you?

We proudly serve students across the United States from our campus in Tewksbury, MA. Requirements differ by state, and our site lists them, Alabama to Wyoming.

Is financial aid available?

For those who qualify: Pell Grants, Federal Loans, Parent PLUS Loans, AmeriCorps, Veterans Administration and Defense Department spousal education. Our school code is 038593.

When does the 30-hour Laser/IPL course run?

Every week, Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday. A reservation is needed: call 978-851-4444 to sign up.

Is the school accredited?

Yes. We're nationally accredited, and our Laser & IPL courses are state approved.

What does a clinic treatment cost?

Electrology is $20 for 15 minutes to an hour. For laser and IPL, come in for a free consultation and we'll give you pricing then.
