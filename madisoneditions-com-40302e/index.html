Services

We handle every step.

More than printing: a partner in selling your work.

  1. Capture

    High-resolution digital capture of your original, in our studio or on-site. Film scanning too.

    A proportional 11 × 14 in proof for your approval before printing. Full-size proofs on request.

Questions

Before you print.

Something else? Call (508) 384-3981.

What can you print from?

Original paintings, photographs, film and digital art. Giclée prints can be made from all of them.

Do I have to bring my painting in?

Most local artists in New England bring their art to our studio. For very large paintings or many works, we can photograph on location at your studio or gallery. From out of state, ship the original to us.

Do I have to print my whole edition at once?

No. Once your proof is approved, prints are made as needed. Your signed-off proof stays on file so every print matches.

Will a reorder next year match?

Yes. We archive your files and keep custom ICC profiles for every camera, monitor, scanner and printer we use, for consistency in every print run.

Can you remove my signature?

Yes. Some artists ask us to, so they can sign each print by hand. It adds value and shows the buyer you approved the print.

Do I get the digital files?

Yes. You receive the finished files for your own use, and we keep a copy so you can reorder any time.
