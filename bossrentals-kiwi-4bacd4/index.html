Good to know

Questions before you book

Anything else? Message our team directly. We're happy to help.

Can I pick up at the airport?

Yes. Collect directly at Christchurch International Airport (CHC) or Queenstown Airport. Locals can also arrange pickup and drop-off across Christchurch and the wider Canterbury region.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Our hire rates include basic insurance, with no hidden fees or surprise charges at pickup.

Are the cars automatic?

Almost all of them, which is ideal if you're not used to New Zealand roads.

Can I book for today?

Yes, same-day bookings are welcome, and you can book online 24/7.

Do you accept debit cards?

Yes, debit cards are accepted.

How long can I hire for?

A day, a weekend, a week or a month. We offer flexible short and long-term hire.

Which car for a ski trip?

For Mount Hutt and other alpine roads, choose one of our AWD or 4WD-capable SUVs. Book ahead, as peak times fill fast.

Ready to explore New Zealand, or just get around Christchurch?

Choose your pick-up, your dates and your trip. Your car will be ready and waiting.
