Founders ask

Straight answers.

What stage do you invest at?

Our founder, Alexander Bechelli, invests in early-stage companies from pre-seed to Series A. Odenn Studio incubates and builds ventures from ideation stage.

What kind of companies does Odenn Ventures back?

Technology companies across enterprise applications, high-tech infrastructure and consumer platforms, with the potential to fundamentally reshape industries.

What is Odenn Impact?

A venture capital fund within a family office. It supports founders solving problems that translate into a meaningful social, enduring and positive cause, guided by the Sustainable Development Goals.

Who does Odenn Studio work with?

VC-backed startup founders, growth businesses and enterprise executives who believe in the future of their business. We work as an embedded Australian and American team.

Where are you based?

Newport Beach in Orange County, California, and Sydney, New South Wales.
