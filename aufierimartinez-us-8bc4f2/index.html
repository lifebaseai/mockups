Fire as craft

A different philosophy of steak

  1. Wood is the flavor

    In asado tradition, wood is not just fuel. Slow-burning embers give the smokiness and texture that define the steak.

  2. Fat is not excess

    Argentine beef is naturally marbled. Over live embers the fat slowly renders, deepening flavor and tenderness.

  3. Time does the rest

    Simple seasoning, natural smoke and patience. We honor the cut as it is meant to be prepared.

Uncompromised. Unrushed. Authentic.

Our history

The ritual of fire

Long before modern steakhouses, the gauchos gathered around wood fires on the Pampas, slowly roasting meat over glowing embers under the open sky. With patience and respect for the flame, they let the meat speak for itself.

At Aufieri & Martínez we honor that heritage with the same philosophy: fire, patience, and quality.

This is more than grilling. It is a ritual passed down through generations.

Good to know

Before you come

Do you ever cook with gas?
No. We cook over natural charcoal and wood. That is the Argentine way.
Is it only steak?
No. Grilled Spanish octopus, PEI mussels, empanadas, sweetbreads, pasta, plus salads, chicken, dishes from the ocean and desserts.
Is it made in house?
Yes. Handcrafted delights, everything homemade.
What are the fries cooked in?
Beef tallow. French, sweet potato, and parmesan with garlic and parsley.
