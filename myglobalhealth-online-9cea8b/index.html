What a video call can do

Six ways Global Health looks after you online

No waiting room, no travel. The doctor sees you by video and writes up what comes next.

  1. Online GP consultations

    A licensed general practitioner by secure video, usually the same or next day.

  2. Specialist consultations

    Cardiologists, psychiatrists, psychologists, dermatologists and other specialists, in your language.

  3. Online treatment

    If the doctor decides treatment is appropriate, your digital treatment plan goes electronically to a local pharmacy.

  4. Medical certificates

    Sick certificates and fitness notes after an online consultation, where the doctor's assessment supports it.

  5. Health and lab tests

    Order blood tests and health checks, then go through the results with a doctor online.

  6. Referrals

    When you need further care, the doctor can write a referral letter to a specialist or hospital.

Questions

What people ask first

Are the doctors licensed in my country?

Yes. You're seen by a doctor registered in the country where you receive care, and every profile shows their registration details.

Can I get treatment or a sick certificate online?

Yes, where the doctor's assessment supports it. Treatment plans are shared electronically for a local pharmacy, and sick certificates and fitness notes can be issued after the consultation.

Which languages do the doctors speak?

Consultations run in 6 languages, depending on which clinicians are available. Each doctor's profile lists the languages they speak. The site itself is in English, Portuguese, Spanish, Czech, Romanian and German.

Is my medical data safe?

Consultations take place by secure video, and your data is protected under the GDPR in the European Union and the LGPD in Brazil.

What is Global Health?

An online medical clinic serving patients in Ireland, Portugal, Spain, Czechia, Romania and Brazil, with doctors registered in each country.

Can I use Global Health in an emergency?

No. Online care does not replace emergency services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
