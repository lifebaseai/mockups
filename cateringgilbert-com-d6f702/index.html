Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else, call or text (480) 420-0007.

What does catering cost?

Packages run $25–$30 (Casual Classics), $35–$40 (Signature Selections) and $45–$65 (Harvest Reserve) per guest, based on 100 guests.

What's included in a package?

1 entrée (9 oz) or 2 entrées (4.5 oz each), plus 2 sides, 1 salad and fresh bread. You choose the entrées, sides, salads and breads.

Where do you cater?

Gilbert, Mesa, Chandler, Tempe, Scottsdale, Phoenix and the surrounding East Valley and Greater Phoenix.

Delivery or full service?

Either. Every package is available for delivery or full-service catering.

Can I build my own menu?

Yes. Mix and match from the à la carte menu, or work with the chef to create a menu of your own.

Do you offer bar service?

Yes, bar and beverage services are part of our menus.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Maricopa Catering Permit # FD-2504064. Family owned and founded in Gilbert in 2006.
