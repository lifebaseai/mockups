Questions project teams ask.

What are your products made from?

Recycled stone, developed using reclaimed stone material. It keeps the strength and timelessness of natural stone with a refined finish and less material waste.

What do you make for hotels?

Guest room essentials, bathroom essentials, architectural wall panels and stone surfaces, so one material can run through a whole space.

Can you make pieces to our design?

Yes. We offer custom manufacturing and work closely with hospitality brands, designers and project teams so every piece aligns with the design vision.

Can you handle a large project?

Yes. We take bulk orders and focus on precision, consistency and scalable execution across projects of varying scale and character.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, we offer global shipping.

Why recycled stone in a hotel?

Durability, ease of maintenance, visual consistency and long-term performance across high-use spaces, with a more sustainable approach to manufacturing.
