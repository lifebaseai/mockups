Questions

Good to know before you book

Can homeopathy help me?

Start with a free 15-minute chat. It's a short, no-obligation call where we talk through your concerns and whether a full consultation makes sense.

How much does it cost?

The first consultation is €80 and follow-up consultations are €50. For patients in Ireland, the cost of the remedies is included in the fee.

What happens at the first consultation?

It lasts 60 to 75 minutes. I ask about your symptoms, how you feel, your medical history and your family medical history. This helps me find the suitable remedy or remedies.

How many appointments will I need?

When there is a recurrent or long-term issue, several consultations spaced roughly four weeks apart are often needed to assess progress and decide what further treatment is required. Follow-ups last 30 to 40 minutes.

Is it safe with my medication?

Yes. Homeopathic treatment can be used safely alongside conventional treatments, and it is safe for babies, pregnant women and people with many allergies and sensitivities.

Can we meet online?

Yes. I work in person in Fermoy, County Cork, and online.
