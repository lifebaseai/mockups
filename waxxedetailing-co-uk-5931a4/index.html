Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Our van brings everything, including 230 litres of pure water, to your home or work, from Abingdon to Marlow, Wokingham, Newbury and everywhere in between. We also detail at our Theale studio.

How far ahead should I book?

We run a busy schedule all year round, so advance bookings are a must.

Will you take on any car?

New cars and daily drivers are welcome, as well as specialist cars. We do not take on neglected cars, so please send photos of your car's current condition when you enquire.

My dealer already coated my new car. Do I need this?

Dealer-offered "coatings" are nothing more than a spray wax. Our Enhancement Detail is the real deal: a machine polish to maximise gloss and remove minor defects, then a ceramic coating to lock it in.

Can you coat my car outdoors?

From April to October, yes. Outside those months we need cover, like a large garage or carport.

Who can book a Maintenance Detail?

Clients who've had one of our packages before. Price depends on the time between details and the distance we travel, so speak to us about regular visits.
