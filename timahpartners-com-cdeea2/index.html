Why now

A looming crisis. A long-term solution.

Small and medium-sized enterprises are the quiet backbone of Southeast Asia. Take Singapore:

99%of businesses are SMEs
70%+of employment comes from SMEs

Thousands of businesses will quietly disappear every year. Timah Partners was built to meet this moment: a permanent, values-driven home for great businesses, and the next generation of leaders to carry them forward.
