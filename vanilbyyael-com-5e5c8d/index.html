    Questions answered

    Before you order.

    Anything else? Message Yael on WhatsApp or text 518-487-8754.

    When should I place my order?

    Orders are accepted until capacity is reached. Order early: the weekly selection often sells out.

    When do you deliver?

    Usually Thursday to Brooklyn and Friday in Lakewood, with some Lakewood deliveries on Thursday depending on the route. You'll be notified in advance. Pickup is in Lakewood, with the address given at checkout.

    What is VANIL's kashrut?

    All desserts are prepared under the hashgacha of KCL Kashrus, following strict kosher standards.

    Can I add a birthday message?

    Yes. Add a golden “Happy Birthday” plaque on the dessert for $5. You can also write a short note at checkout, and it's printed and included with your order at no charge.

    Can I send a dessert as a gift?

    Yes. Place the order for someone else and include a note. Just make sure the delivery or pickup details are filled out accurately.

    Do you accommodate allergies?

    Some desserts may contain nuts, gluten, dairy or other allergens. Ingredients are listed, but we can't guarantee an allergen-free environment.

    How do I order a custom cake?

    Send your event details on WhatsApp or through the cake inquiry form above. Custom orders are handled separately from the weekly menu.

    Can I change or cancel my order?

    Because desserts are prepared fresh, changes and cancellations aren't guaranteed. Reach out as soon as possible and we'll do our best.
