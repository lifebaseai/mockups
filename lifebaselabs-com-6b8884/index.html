    Hey, I’m Sunny GSole creator of Life Base

    I have been a decade long user of OneNote. Well over thousands of pages, tens of notebooks, hundreds of folders. It became an extension of me.

    Over the years I felt limited by it, and found myself using many apps alongside it to manage my life. So I created Life Base: a one-stop second brain, an extension of yourself.

    What you see is just the start. Over time, you will see hundreds of potential apps you can use to build your life and your mind, more beautiful and more intelligent over time.
