The farm

Sigma Estates, Kotagiri.

An organic farm in a quietly tucked away hill station in the Western Ghats of the Nilgiris. It grew out of our love of travel, permaculture, farm stays and good food.

The whole farm was laid out with permaculture design. We farm naturally, use natural resources and keep traditional knowledge alive: a road back to nature.
