The ride

From the station to the bottom of the holl'r.

Riders control their speed: take it slow for the mountain views, or go for the thrill.

  1. 1

    Buckle in at the station

    You're seated in a cart, buckled in and checked for safety by our crew.

  2. 2

    Climb 700 feet

    Your cart climbs straight up the mountain, with the West Virginia hills opening up around you.

  3. 3

    A small drop to the left

    Then the real excitement begins.

  4. 4

    2,500 feet of curves

    Race through hairpin curves and 360-degree turns at up to 30 mph, as fast or as easy as you like.

  5. 5

    Again?

    Back at the station, stop by our gift shop or head right back for another ride. Extra rides are just $10, $5 for ages 3 to 6.

Length & design

Built for these hills.

The track winds through the forest with a coal mine theme that reflects the region's heritage.

  • Custom-built in Germany, assembled on site by the manufacturer
  • State-of-the-art safety features and easy controls
  • Opened in 2025

FAQs

Before you come.

Still wondering about something? Give us a call at (304) 712-7055.

Do I need a reservation?

No reservations required. Buy tickets ahead or in person at the station.

Is it OK for kids?

Yes. It's built with state-of-the-art safety features and easy controls, so it's as much fun for kids and families as it is for thrill-seekers. Riders must meet all height and age requirements and follow the posted safety guidelines.

How fast does it go?

Up to 30 mph. Each rider controls their own speed, so you can take it easy and enjoy the views or let it run.

What if it rains?

We run rain or shine. Thunder or lightning may pause operations.

Where are you?

Just a short drive from the Princeton, WV exit, right off the interstate. Get directions.

Is there a waiver?

Yes. Sign the waiver on our website.
