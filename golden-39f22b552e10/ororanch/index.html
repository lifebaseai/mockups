How Oro Ranch got its name

OROSpanish for gold. Andy is a geologist who has spent his whole career exploring for it in Mexico, Peru and Ecuador.

In the late 1800s, my great, great grandfather came west from Missouri and settled near Alberton, drawn by the power and beauty of the Rocky Mountain front.

His grandson ran a small sawmill. My dad followed him into the wood products industry, and his work took our family around Montana, including Seeley Lake, just down the road from here.

Waking up each day in our “Last Best Place”, I feel like I've come full circle. We are truly blessed to have a piece of paradise, and we love sharing it with others.

Come see us soon, Deborah and Andy Carstensen

Where is Oro Ranch?

1420 Flint Ridge Road, Condon, Montana 59826, in the northwest corner of the state. Open in Google Maps

Do you rent the whole ranch?

Yes. We rent the whole ranch for weddings and events, rather than one-night stays, so it's yours alone. Your guests can stay over in the lodge, with room for up to 31.

How many guests can you host?

Intimate weddings and elopements of up to 50 guests, with room for up to 31 of them to stay over.

Can we film or photograph here?

Yes. The entire property and nearly all of its contents are available, from vast panoramic landscapes to intimate indoor and outdoor spaces.

What can our guests do?

Wet a fly in the babbling stream, stroll in lush meadows, or blaze new trails through the forest, and keep an eye out for eagles, deer, elk and bears.
