Yes. We work with startups, SMEs and established companies, from new founders to multinational enterprises.

We handle company setup and incorporation, including offshore companies. We hold agreements with Meydan, RAKEZ, SHAMS and Ajman NuVentures, and a business licence with SPC.

Yes. Bank account opening and bank loans are part of our banking services, along with corporate insurance.

Yes. We guide you through ICV, ISO and HACCP, plus trade name and trademark registration.
