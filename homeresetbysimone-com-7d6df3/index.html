Hi, I'm Simone.

I started The Home Reset by Simone because I believe everyone deserves a home that brings peace, not stress.

Working with individuals with ADHD and autism showed me that an organized home isn't just about appearances. It's about creating systems that support everyday life.

So I don't just organize homes. I design personalized, functional systems that are easy to maintain and tailored to the way you naturally live.

Whether you're overwhelmed by clutter, settling into a new home, preparing for a major life transition, or simply ready for a fresh start, my team and I are here to help you reclaim your space.

Serving Baltimore, Baltimore County, Howard County, and Anne Arundel County, Maryland.

Questions

Before you book.

How do I get started?

Book a free 1-hour virtual consultation. We'll learn your goals, routines, and challenges, and help you pick the right reset.

What does a reset cost?

Hourly resets start at $375 for up to 3 hours. Reset Elite and our move-in and move-out concierge resets start at $2,000, and the In-Home Laundry Reset is $200. Final pricing is confirmed after your complimentary consultation.

Where do you work?

In person across Baltimore City, Baltimore County, Howard County, and Anne Arundel County, Maryland.

Will you judge my home?

Never. Judgment-free support for whatever season you're in, from overwhelmed moms to post-move or post-chaos.

Do you work with ADHD?

Yes. We design brain-friendly systems around how you actually think, with visual cues and simple routines that stick.

How does it stay organized afterward?

Every reset includes a maintenance guide. We also share practical maintenance strategies and access to the Home Reset Companion App.
