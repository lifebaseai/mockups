The NICHOLAS AIR Story

The best, not the biggest.

We have never aspired to be a value brand. We are a luxury brand, defined by excellence, craftsmanship and quality over quantity.

Questions

Before you fly with us

How many hours does a Jet Card cover?

From 15 to 100 hours, with four flexible membership options and a minimal upfront commitment.

What if I fly more than 100 hours a year?

Jet Lease is built for you: predictable monthly payments and no upfront asset commitment.

Can I own part of an aircraft?

Yes. Fractional Ownership gives you greater flexibility at a fraction of the cost, without the hassles of whole aircraft ownership.

Who helps me choose?

Your Personal Travel Representative, standing by to help you choose the right program and send brochures and pricing.
