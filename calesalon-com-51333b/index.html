Questions

Good to know before you come in

When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We are closed Sunday and Monday.

Why do prices say “& up”?

All prices are subject to change depending on the length and texture of your hair.

Do you do bridal hair and makeup?

Yes. Brides start at $100, bridesmaids at $75, and make-up at $65.

How much is balayage, ombre or extensions?

These are priced upon consultation. Call us at (732) 548-5666 or stop by the salon.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Kids haircuts (12 and under) start at $30, and kids updos start at $50.
