The Barna Bee history

It started in Europe. It continues on Long Island.

Barna Bee is a family tradition of bee keeping that began in Europe nearly a century ago. Today the Barna family keeps those traditions and methods going on Long Island, New York, alongside New World advances.

Sustainable beekeeping, hand harvesting and a lot of hard work. The goal hasn't changed: Long Island's greatest honey.

A pure expression of the unique climate and flowers of Long Island.
