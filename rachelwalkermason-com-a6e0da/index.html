Questions

Before you get in touch

I only have half a song. Is that enough?

Yes. In one artist’s words: “I bring her a half-finished idea and she’s able to step into the song and work out immediately what it needs.”

What kind of music does Rachel write?

Pop, country, rock, jazz, k-pop, R&B and folk, for herself and many other artists. She has also written award-winning songs for charities and to commemorate special events across the UK.

Who does Rachel coach?

Hundreds of singers so far, from beginners to West End performers.

Can Rachel judge or speak at our event?

She has judged the ICCAs, Masters of Show Choir, the World Choir Games, Showchoir Canada and The Voice Festival, and judges alongside Michael Eavis at Glastonbury Festival’s Snowdrop Songwriting Festival. Her feedback comes in her trademark quirky and uplifting style.

Where can I hear Rachel’s music?

All of Rachel’s music is on Spotify, and on YouTube at @listenincolourwithrachel.
