Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you deliver to me?

We deliver to all SP1, SP2, SP4 and SP5 postcodes. Outside those areas, you're welcome to collect. Check your postcode.

Is there a minimum order or delivery charge?

Delivery is on orders of £20 or more. There's a £5 charge for all deliveries to SP4 and SP5.

When can I order a takeaway?

Between 5pm and 9pm, Monday to Sunday.

Can I choose my delivery or collection time?

Yes. Add your preferred time in the order comments field at checkout and we'll always do our best to fit it.

I have a food allergy. Can you help?

Please contact the restaurant in advance on 01722 327628 so we can advise and accommodate where possible.

Are you open every day?

Yes, seven days a week. Monday to Thursday 12–3pm and 5–9pm, Friday to Sunday 12–9pm.
