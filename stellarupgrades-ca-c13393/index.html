    How much does solar cost in Edmonton?

    Most homes land between roughly $14,000 (3 kW) and $50,000+ (15–30 kW). Our $2.80/W cash price applies to a typical 7–8 kW install. Smaller systems carry a per-watt premium because fixed costs spread over fewer panels: a 5 kW runs $16,000–$20,000 depending on the roof. $0-down financing is available.

    Does solar work in Alberta winters?

    Yes. Edmonton gets 2,300+ hours of sunshine a year, more than Toronto, Montreal or Vancouver, and cold temperatures actually improve panel efficiency. Net metering banks your summer surplus to offset winter use, so a properly sized system is designed for a $0 annual power bill on a seasonal solar plan.
