Questions

The short answers

How long do I have to stay?

The Villa is let by the month, with a one-month minimum. The Lanai is booked by the season, with a two-month minimum. Need something odd-length or split? Ask us.

Why is it cheaper than the booking sites?

Booking sites add roughly 12–15% at checkout. Book straight with us and that goes away. Pay by bank transfer and we cover that cost too.

What comes with the house?

Both homes are inside the gated Gran Paradiso community, so both come with the resort pool and spa, tennis and pickleball, the fitness centre with sauna and steam room, and the 24-hour gate.

How many people does each house sleep?

Both sleep six. The Villa has three bedrooms and two baths; The Lanai has four bedrooms and two baths.

Who do I talk to?

Us, the owners. We answer email the same day and the phone most of the time.
