good to knowBefore you come in

When is happy hour?

11:00 am to 5:00 pm. Oysters of the day are $2 each, and 16 oz Harbour lager or amber ale is 7.5.

When can we get a seafood boil?

After 5:00 pm, for 2 or 4. Mussels & clams is 50 per person; lobster is 75 per person.

Which oysters are on today?

They change. Ask about our fresh oysters of the day.

Is there a late-night list?

From 9:00 pm to close: 16 oz Harbour lager or amber ale 7.5, highballs 7, wine 8.5, tequila shots 5.

Coming with a group?

A 20% gratuity applies to groups of 6 or more. Send us a note below or call 604.251.6900.
