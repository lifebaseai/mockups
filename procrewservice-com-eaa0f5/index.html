Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else, call 602-653-9277.

Do you manage aircraft now?

We're building it now, on top of our crew work. Send us your aircraft brief and we'll talk it through.

What qualifications do your pilots hold?

Our Citation pilots hold a valid ATP licence and a type rating for the specific model they fly. Every pilot holds valid licences and certifications for their aircraft.

Which aircraft do you cover?

The Citation family (CJ1 to CJ4, XLS and XLS+, Latitude, Sovereign, X and X+) and Part 91 jet captain roles.

What is a contract pilot?

A professional pilot hired for a specific assignment, like a one-time flight, a ferry flight or a relief role, with no long-term commitment.

Contract or full-time: which do I need?

Contract suits a single flight or a set assignment. Full-time suits a permanent position. We do both.

Can you help on short notice?

Yes. We provide qualified crew on short notice for corporate flight departments, private jet owners and charter operators.
