Stay curious

Everything you always wanted to know

What is crowdfunding?

Many people finance a project with small amounts, through an open call on the internet. All these small funders are called the crowd. That's different from a bank loan, where one or a few investors put in a large amount.

How does a campaign work?
  1. You (a person, collective, entrepreneur…) contact Growfunding with your project.
  2. You launch a call to the general public on Growfunding.
  3. People finance the project with a contribution on Growfunding.
Do I need a finished plan?

No. Whether you have a wild idea or a well-thought-out plan, Growfunding offers custom support for your project, from the basics of civic crowdfunding to customised advice.

What do growfunders get back?

Depending on the type of crowdfunding, you may or may not receive a symbolic, material or financial reward in exchange for your contribution. On Growfunding, projects have given 56.471 rewards so far.

Can I get extra support for my campaign?

Yes. Growfunding runs thematic calls for projects with organizations, companies and local governments. Check which call matches your project, like "Realize your circular dream" with the KBS, open till 30/12/2026.

Can I support a project long-term?

With Spacefunding: monthly and annual options to support your favourite projects and help them create a lasting community.
