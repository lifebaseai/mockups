Why choose us

A sanctuary built for the whole woman

  1. Expert, compassionate care

    Our highly skilled midwifery team provides evidence-based, high-quality care with a warm and personal touch.

  2. Empowerment

    Care built around you. We support informed decisions and invite you to take an active part in your care.

Meet the founder

Rachelle Molière-Ogunlana, CNM, FNP

She pictured a place where a woman could walk in as a teenager for her first well-woman visit and still be coming back decades later.

Rachelle built Clinique Molière because she believed women in Southern California deserved more than fragmented, rushed care. She is the founder and Clinical Director, board-certified as both a Nurse-Midwife and a Family Nurse Practitioner. Very few practitioners in Southern California hold both.

12+years of experience
500+births
2board certifications

Education: University of Miami and Frontier Nursing University.

Certifications: Newborn Resuscitation Provider, ACLS, BLS, prescriptive privileges, DEA licensure, Placenta Encapsulation Specialist, Spinning Babies, and Hypno-Birthing Mongan Method instructor.

Questions

What women ask us first

Can I give birth at Clinique Molière?

Yes. We are California's largest freestanding birth center, with peaceful birthing suites for out-of-hospital birth.

Do you take insurance?

We are in-network with leading insurance providers, including Aetna and Cigna.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday, 9am to 4pm, closed 12 to 1pm for lunch. Weekends by appointment only.

Where are you?

735 South Brea Boulevard, Brea, CA 92821. We serve women from three counties.

What can I come in for?

Well-woman care, acupuncture, chiropractic, yoga, birth and postpartum care, monitrice and doula care, education, breastfeeding support, placenta encapsulation and postpartum wellness.

Who will care for me?

Our midwifery team, led by our founder and Clinical Director, Rachelle Molière-Ogunlana, CNM, FNP.
