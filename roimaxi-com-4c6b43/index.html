Questions

Straight answers.

What does it cost?

A one-off setup of $1,000–$3,000, then $200–$500 per qualified meeting that took place. Or a flat $5,000 a month. Your exact price inside the range is set on the first call.

What if a prospect doesn't show up?

You pay nothing for it. A meeting that no-shows is not counted. Whether no-shows and reschedules are replaced, credited or absorbed is decided up front, not argued afterwards.

Is there a minimum term?

No. There is no minimum term on either way of paying.

Who decides what “qualified” means?

You do, with us, in writing, before outreach starts: seniority, company size and sector, budget authority, expressed need, held not booked, and how no-shows are handled.

How do you reach people?

Cold email, AI-personalized to each prospect's role, company and industry challenges, sent to a verified, enriched list built around your ICP, with follow-up sequences.

Who do you work with?

B2B companies in the UK and US, and their sales, RevOps and go-to-market teams.

What will I see while it runs?

A weekly report: emails sent, replies, positive replies and meetings that took place.
