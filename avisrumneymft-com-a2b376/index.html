I can help you

Maybe this sounds familiar.

  • “I should be able to figure this out myself.”
  • “Maybe it’s not such a big deal.”
  • The same thoughts, over and over, without getting any clearer.

Do you overeat or drink too much, or spend money unwisely when you are stressed? Or are you upset because you witness these behaviors in a loved one?

Then you criticize yourself, or get down, because you can’t “fix” what is bothering you.

You don’t need to struggle with this alone. Talking with someone knowledgeable, empathic and unbiased, who has your best interests at heart, can help.

I will help you turn self-sabotaging habits and self-critical thinking into self-affirming behaviors that build your confidence, improve your relationships and increase your well-being.

— Avis

Emotional Eating

Food doesn’t have to be how you cope.

Eating to feel better is not an unusual phenomenon. But do you routinely use food to cope, and then feel your eating is out of control?

Avis has particular expertise in helping people recover from emotional eating, undereating, overeating and binging, and from the relationship patterns that feed them. She also brings her own experience of recovery from an eating disorder.

Meet Avis

Warm, compassionate, and on your side.

Avis Rumney, LMFT, is a master therapist and award-winning author who has helped hundreds of clients create happier, healthier lives. She works with individuals, couples and families, knowing that many of the problems people bring to therapy are rooted in past and present relationship patterns.

She believes that within each of us is the desire to heal and grow, and that with guidance and support you can find a way of being that honors who you are.
