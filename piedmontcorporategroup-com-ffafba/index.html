04  Questions

Quick answers.

The short version, before you send anything over.

Where do I send a wholesale dealer application?
Straight to our procurement team. Use the form below, or email purchasing@piedmontcorporategroup.com.
What else does the procurement team handle?
Distribution catalogs and vendor onboarding compliance paperwork, along with commercial account inquiries.
Is every product really inspected?
Yes. 100% manual inbound inspection and logistical preparation, at our dedicated local facility.
How do you protect our brand and pricing?
With strict adherence to pricing regulations and vendor policies. They guide every marketplace scaling decision we make.
Who do you work with?
Leading manufacturers and domestic wholesale distributors. We align with them directly.
When is the team available?
Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
