Plain answers

Do I pay a brokerage fee as a buyer?

No. We charge buyers no brokerage fees, and we put you in direct contact with the property owner.

Does the 2027 tax change affect me?

If you are a non-EU buyer, yes: transfer tax rises from 3% to 15% on 1 January 2027. On a €1,000,000 property that is €150,000 instead of €30,000.

Where in Greece do you have properties?

Santorini, Mykonos, Athens, Crete, Halkidiki, Corfu, Lefkada, Zakynthos, Paros, Naxos, Rhodes, Porto Heli and other Greek islands.

Do you have properties for the Golden Visa?

Yes. We list apartments and properties for the Golden Visa in Greece, including apartments in Athens.

Can you help with the legal side?

Yes. Legal coverage is part of how we support a purchase, with senior attorney Maria Papadaki.
