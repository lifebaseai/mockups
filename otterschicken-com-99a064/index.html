Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Anything else? Call us at (770) 428-8333.

Is the chicken really fresh?

Yes. 100% all-natural chicken, prepared fresh and never frozen, cooked in soybean oil. Fried, grilled or tossed in sauce. We make everything ourselves, even the famous Otter’s Sauce.

When is breakfast?

Wednesday through Sunday, 7 AM to 11 AM. Lunch and dinner start at 11 AM every day.

Can you cater our event?

Yes. We have breakfast catering and lunch & dinner catering, from chicken biscuits and waffle trays to tender and nugget trays. Use the planner above, or call us to plan your order.

Do you help with fundraisers?

Our local schools and sports teams are at the heart of our business. Call us if you’re interested in partnership or fundraising opportunities.

Where are you?

We’re at The Avenue West Cobb: 3625 Dallas Hwy SW, Suite 690, Marietta, GA 30064.
