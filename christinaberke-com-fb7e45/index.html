About Christina

Christina Berke is a Chilean-American writer and trauma-informed educator based in the Los Angeles area (Chumash territory).

Her fiction manuscript, based on her maternal family’s history during the 1973 military coup in Chile, won Honorable Mention for the 2022 Miami Book Fair Emerging Writers Fellowship. A former Managing Editor for Black Mountain Institute’s literary magazine Witness, she has also served as a juried reader for writing contests and residencies.

She loves dogs and sound baths.
