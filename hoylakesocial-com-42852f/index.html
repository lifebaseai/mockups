Good to know

Your questions, answered.

When are you open?

Tuesday to Saturday from 7pm until late. We're closed on Sundays and Mondays.

How much is room hire?

Room hire starts from £75. Call 0151 632 3035 with your date and numbers.

How many people can we fit?

Up to 140 seated, and the lounge holds 50.

Can we bring our own food?

Yes. Own catering is welcome, and the PA system is included.

Can I play snooker on a Tuesday?

The tables are open during opening hours except Tuesday evenings, when it's bingo. Contact Andy Thornton to book a table.

Is there parking?

Yes, safe off-road parking at the club on Trinity Road.
