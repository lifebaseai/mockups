Our story

A global outlook, from the very start.

Our mission: help our clients cross borders and build bridges.

Our founder, Deep SenGupta, spent his childhood between England, India and Canada. Those years shaped his global outlook early.

He came to the USA for graduate legal studies, then started a consulting group within one of the world’s leading transportation companies. There he helped over a thousand importers and exporters, every one with one foot in the USA and the other in a foreign country.

DSG Global carries that on: to be the preferred guide for importers and exporters to global trade rules and tariffs around the world.

How do I get started?

Book a free initial consultation, or take one of our 25-question assessments first. Each takes less than 10 minutes.

Which assessment is right for me?

The Export Compliance Score shows where your export compliance stands. The International Trade Compliance Score shows your company’s trade compliance risk areas.

Who do you work with?

Importers and exporters of all sizes, from small and medium-sized companies to the Fortune 500.

What do you help with?

International trade compliance, global business strategy and training. We help you understand complex trade rules and expand your global footprint.

Are you a law firm or a CPA firm?

Neither. DSG Global is a management consulting firm. We don’t give legal advice, and we don’t provide attest services or audits.

Can I learn on my own first?

Yes. Watch our free videos on the DSG Global YouTube channel, and sign up for Trade Insights, “Deep Thoughts on International Trade”.
