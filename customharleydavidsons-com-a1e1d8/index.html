\\\\The shop, since 2005

Form must follow function.

If a bike can’t be ridden, it doesn’t make much sense to build it. So every one of ours is a rider: a bike you’re proud to throw your leg over and twist the throttle down an open backroad.

One of a kind, with style and function, for riders across the Southeast, the US and abroad. We’re also the in-house custom builder for No Limit Motorsports and build all of their Harleys.

\\\\Straight answers

Before you call

I’m not close to Belmont. Can I still buy one?
Yes. Nationwide and worldwide shipping is available, and our riders live across the Southeast, the US and other countries.
How do I make a bike mine?
Call or text 704-408-1024 or 704-913-9767, or send the form below.
Can you build one for me?
Yes. We’ve built bikes for every type of client. See past customer builds on our current site.
Where’s the shop?
111 Hubbard St, Belmont, NC 28012. Open in Maps.
Do you sell parts and apparel?
Yes. The online parts store and SECC apparel are on customharleydavidsons.com.
What do “Level 2” and “Level 3” mean?
They’re our engine packages. Each listing names its package, and its torque and horsepower where given.
