Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else, call AMG at (508) 842-1900.

What can AMG sell for me?

Residential and commercial real estate, business liquidations, estate sales and personal property. AMG is a full service auction and real estate company, and also does valuations.

Who does AMG work for?

Individuals, trustees, businesses, public agencies and institutions. AMG is an approved vendor with various Federal agencies and has performed services for FDIC and HUD.

Where do you work?

Throughout New England. Licences: MA LIC #207, NH LIC #5018, VT LIC #057-0002433, Maine LIC #AUC1727.

How do you find buyers?

Each sale gets its own marketing strategy, built to expose the property to as many potential and informed buyers as possible.

Do you run charity auctions?

Yes. Dale Schaetzke personally conducts lively benefit auctions for charities and non-profits.
