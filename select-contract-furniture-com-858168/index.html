Good to know

Your questions, answered.

Do I need an appointment to visit the showroom?

Yes, showroom visits are by appointment only. Plan your visit above or call us on 01355 26 36 49 and we'll promptly respond.

What if you don't have what I'm looking for?

Trust us to source it for you. We're also exclusive distributors for several premier hospitality furniture manufacturers.

Are your fabrics flame retardant?

Yes. All fabrics we offer meet current flame retardancy legislation, and we can provide certification as required.

Will you work with our architect or designer?

Happily. Many leading interior designers and architects already specify with us.

Do you design and install as well?

Yes. We offer bespoke design and installation, a full design and planning service, and interior joinery: a complete turnkey furnishing package.
