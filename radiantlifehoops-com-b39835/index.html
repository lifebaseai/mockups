Laura Loops & Radiant Life Hoops

Why I make hoops.

Years ago I lived in Cambodia. I was already a hooper, but the only hoops were bamboo with plastic ribbon: light, but they broke easily and couldn’t travel. And I wasn’t the only one on the beach who wanted to play.

So I learned the Khmer words for “tube” and “connector”, went to the hardware store, and came home with heavy orange tubing and giant sheets of motorbike sparkle tape. Days of cutting, and a pot of boiling water to get the connectors in.

Today Radiant Life is my full time work, and my home is Vancouver, my favourite city.

  • MovementTake time to move and play daily.
  • Inspired friendsSurround yourself with them. Lift each other up!
  • FreedomMake time to pursue your passions.

Certified Hoop Play instructor and teacher trainer. Full time since 2017. Classes in Vancouver and Kelowna.

Good to know

Your questions, answered.

More in the hoop size and care guides, or just ask Laura.

Which size hoop is right for me?
Check the hoop size guide for the different sizes and tubings, or tell Laura about yourself when you send your hoop. She’ll go through what size works for you.
Can I pick up, or do you ship?
Both. Order pickups are available in Vancouver, and hoops ship within Canada.
Waiting for pickup details or tracking?
Laura will contact you with pickup details or your tracking number. Don’t forget to check your junk email.
My new hoop looks bent. What do I do?
Your polypro or HDPE hoop leaves the workshop in a perfect circle. Let it breathe overnight so it can reform. Still bent or kinked after relaxing? Contact Laura: it’s meant to be a circle!
When do classes run?
October to June, in person in Vancouver, so you can keep spinning through all the rainy days.
