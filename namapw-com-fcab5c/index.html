Our story

Ali Farooq and Saad Zariff

It started in Los Angeles, with a business meeting at Porto's. Saad was working for an ethical, faith-focused wealth management firm. Ali was working for an ethical mortgage company while managing assets for his Muslim clients.

The meeting became a friendship, and over nearly a decade, a shared vision: to give back to their community and make a positive difference in the lives of those around them. Then one text message, and Nama Private Wealth began.

A firm designed to help Millennials, Gen Z and Gen Y build a healthy understanding of wealth: how to grow it, how to keep it, and how to live in the present while planning for the future.
