Good questions.

Short answers. Anything else, call 800-556-7264.

Where can I buy your tile?
Metal Medley is available directly from us at MTT. Our other two lines are sold through Daltile and Florida Tile.
What metals do you work with?
Sustainable metals. Our Daltile line is iron and bronze colored; our Florida Tile line is aluminum and solid metal.
Is it only for homes?
No. Our designs suit any interior space, from residential to commercial to large hospitality environments.
Accent or a whole wall?
Metal Medley adds sparkle to any space, whether it's just an accent or an entire wall. Our Daltile line offers perfect accents that add shine, and our Florida Tile line is perfect to accent any backsplash, bar or bathroom.
