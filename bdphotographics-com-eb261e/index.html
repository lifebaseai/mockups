About Barry

A life spent studying light.

Barry DiBernardo travels the world photographing some of its most iconic and remote settings.

As a young professional photographer, he noticed his clients kept asking him to retouch their photos. Curious and empathetic, he wondered if those flaws could be solved for good. That led him to become Dr. Barry DiBernardo, a world-renowned plastic surgeon, still guided by what light can do.

Can I buy a print?

Yes. Barry sells prints of his work, including Yosemite National Park and French Polynesia. You can ask about any photograph on this page, right below.

Is this the same Dr. Barry DiBernardo?

Yes. Photography came first. His clients' wish to look their best led him to plastic surgery, and light is still at the heart of both.
