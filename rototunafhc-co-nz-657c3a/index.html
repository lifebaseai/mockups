About us

“We have created a practice that we believe in and would choose for our own family members.”

Rototuna Family Health Centre gives personalised, high-quality care, whenever you need it or before you do. Feel free to come in and visit our friendly team at any time.

Your whole family

Care for every age, helping everyone focus on their own wellness and independence.

Every culture, respected

Warm, inclusive, friendly care that acknowledges different cultures, in a safe and respectful way.

Prevention first

Preventing disease and promoting healthy lifestyles, with accurate diagnosis and proactive management.

New to us?

Your first visit, made easy.

  1. Enrol with us

    Complete a registration form before you come, or let us know you’d like to join.

  2. Bring a few things

    A form of ID, your completed registration form, and your medical insurance card if you have one.

  3. Take your time

    Your first doctor’s appointment is a double appointment, so we get a chance to know you better.

Questions we’re often asked

How do I book an appointment?
Call us on 07 853 6586, or email contactus@rototunafhc.co.nz.
Do you accept walk-ins?
You can walk in, but we can’t guarantee an appointment. We recommend calling ahead to secure a time with our doctors.
What if I need help after hours?
Call our virtual doctor on 027 222 8182, Monday to Friday 6pm–12am and weekends 8am–8pm. Otherwise call 111, or a 24-hour clinic: Anglesea Clinic, Gate 1, Cnr Thackeray & Anglesea Street, 07 858 0800; Tui Medical, 26 Bryant Road, Te Rapa, 0800 175 175.
Do you offer specialist referrals?
Yes. Please speak to your GP about this.
Is there disabled parking?
Yes, we have disabled parking on site.
