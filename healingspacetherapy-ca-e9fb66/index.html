Our approach

Holistic, evidence-based, and trauma-informed.

Every therapist here is trauma-informed, with additional training in trauma. We prioritize your safety, build trust, and meet you exactly where you are.

  1. 01

    Grounded in research

    EMDR, IFS, EFT, ACT, CBT and more, all evidence-based treatments.

  2. 02

    The relationship comes first

    Healing happens in safe, trusting relationships, so that's where we begin.

  3. 03

    You set the pace

    Progress and goals are shaped collaboratively throughout our work.

  4. 04

    Healing at the root

    We move beyond managing symptoms to heal the deeper patterns, experiences and wounds beneath them.

Good to know

Questions, answered gently.

The things people often ask before starting. Still wondering something? Reach out any time.

How do I get started?

Ask for a free 15-minute consultation using the short form below. We'll help you find the right therapist and a time that works.

How long are sessions, and what do they cost?

Sessions are 60 minutes. Fees range from $160 to $200 depending on the therapist; you can see each one's fee above.

Can I see someone online?

Yes. Every therapist on our team also offers secure virtual sessions, available anywhere in Ontario.

Who will I be working with?

Registered psychotherapists and social workers. Every therapist here is trauma-informed, with additional training in trauma.

Is this for emergencies?

No. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline) or 9-1-1.
