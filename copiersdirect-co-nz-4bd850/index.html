Before you call.

Who finances the lease?

We do. We lease our own machines, so there's no third-party finance company in the middle.

What's included each month?

Fixed monthly costs with cost per print maintenance plans. Servicing, support and consumables are handled for you.

Will you set it up for us?

Yes. Installation and setup on your network, with the correct drivers and scanning, is included in our delivery.

Can you look at the lease we have now?

Yes. We offer a no-obligation assessment of your current devices and lease.

Which brands do you supply?

We compare major brands including Hewlett Packard, Canon and Toshiba, and also work with Brother.

Do we have to lease?

No. We recommend leasing or purchasing, whichever works best for you.
