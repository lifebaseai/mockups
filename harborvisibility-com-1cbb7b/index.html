      FAQ

      Questions therapists often ask

      Quick answers to help you decide if this is a fit.

      Can this reduce reliance on Psychology Today?

      The goal is to build independent visibility through your own website. Many therapists keep using directories, but with stronger organic visibility they are no longer fully dependent on them.

      How long does it take to build independent visibility?

      You start showing up in Google in month 1, first inquiries often come in months 2 to 3, and the most progress usually happens between months 6 and 12.

      Does it work for solo practices and group practices?

      Both. It’s the same underlying approach, structured differently depending on whether search needs to find one clinician or several.

      Do you guarantee rankings or a number of clients?

      No. Ethical visibility work does not involve guarantees. The focus is alignment, structure, and steady improvement over time.
