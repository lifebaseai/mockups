Questions, answered

Do you only work with Victoria businesses?

No. Jesse is based in Victoria, BC, and works with businesses across Canada and beyond.

Isn’t branding just a logo?

A logo is just the beginning. Branding turns your business into one coherent visual system, so it feels consistent whether someone sees your storefront, business card, packaging or social media.

Will it work on signs, merch and packaging?

That’s the point. The logo work is built to hold up everywhere your brand lives: signage, packaging, merch, vehicles, websites and print.

Why does print need its own designer?

Because print is where craft still matters. Menus, posters, brochures and cards have to hold up in the real world: on walls, in hands, on tables and in shop windows. If it’s going to be printed, it should be designed by someone who actually understands print.
