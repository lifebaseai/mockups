What a wall can do

Colour changes how a place feels, and how people act in it.

Round things, pops of bright color, symmetrical shapes, lightness or elevation all innately bring us joy.Quest Nine

  • In offices

    Researchers have found people in more colourful offices are more alert, confident, friendly and productive.

  • In schools and restaurants

    Attendance and clientele improve.

  • In public spaces

    Graffiti disappears.

  • For the people who live there

    Quest Nine’s focus is a more connected community, and residents and youth left with hope, joy and a sense of inclusion.

About the artist

Meet Q.

Kori ‘Q’ Thomas is an artist and designer living in New England. She started creating after a profound healing experience with art therapy in 2015.

Her style grew from a passion for street art while living in Detroit for most of her 20s. Today she is a travelling artist, and her aim is simple: to draw people out of their everyday lives for a moment, into the present.

Beyond murals, she designs clothing for her brand Satori Soul Society, illustrates and writes a children’s book series, and is raising her young son, Theo.

Look for the glitches. She adds them to her work. They stand for the unpredictability of life itself: bursts of energy, and fleeting moments of joy, pain and emotion.

Questions

Before you write

What kinds of places do you paint?

Restaurants and bars, schools and youth groups, city barriers and memorials, mural festivals, a warehouse and apartments. The portfolio above shows each one.

Will you travel to my city?

Kori is based in New England and is a travelling artist. She has painted murals in Northern and Southern California, the Pacific Northwest, Flint and Detroit, Atlanta, Nashville, Miami and Montreal.

How big can a mural be?

Her walls run from an 8′ × 8′ piece at Drip Back Yard Bar to 40′ × 12′ at The Joint of Miami for Art Basel, which she completed in 3 days. Try your own size in the wall planner.

What are the “glitches” in your work?

They stand for the unpredictability of life itself: bursts of energy, and fleeting moments of joy, pain and emotion.

Do you take canvas commissions?

Yes, a limited number. Email quest9design@gmail.com with your interest.
