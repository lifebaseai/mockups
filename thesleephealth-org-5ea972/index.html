How can we help?

Still have a question?

Short answers here. Or call us toll free at (866) 808-5785.

Are the parent guides free?

Yes. Free guides written by clinicians, sorted by your child's age, plus a free pediatric melatonin guide.

What ages do you cover?

Ages 0 to 18: infants (0 to 12 months), toddlers (1 to 5 years), school age (6 to 12 years) and teens (13 to 18 years).

How do I know when to see a doctor?

Our guides cover signs worth asking your doctor about, and when to ask your clinician for a referral.

Who is SHIP® for?

Sleep consultants, physicians, occupational therapists and allied health practitioners. There are tracks for pediatricians and for sleep consultants.

Is there free CME?

Yes. The ADHD and Sleep CME recording is free.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is educational content only, not medical advice or a clinical diagnosis. Consult your child's physician before changing care. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
