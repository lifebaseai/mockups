    Questions

    What families ask us

    Can I reach someone at night?

    Yes. We can help 24/7. Call (816) 232-3366 at any hour.

    How do I watch a service online?

    Open the obituary, choose “Obituary & Events”, find “Live Webcast” and choose “Watch Event”. The service starts at the time published.

    Can we choose cremation without a service?

    Yes. A direct cremation is affordable and straightforward. You can also choose cremation with a memorial ceremony.

    Is everything in one place?

    Yes. With our on-site crematory and reception center, your loved ones only have to make one stop.

    Can I visit before deciding anything?

    Yes. Meet with a member of our team to tour the facility and get to know our staff.

    Can planning ahead include military honors?

    Yes. Planning ahead covers decisions like military honors and veteran services, as well as caskets, urns, memorials and keepsake jewelry.

    Do you serve my town?

    We serve St. Joseph, Savannah, Avenue City, Agency, Clarksdale, Faucett, Country Club, DeKalb, Cosby and Union Star.
