About Dricrete

There had to be a better way.

2001Supplying the construction industry

Leadership: Tim Yeiser, founder

Our founder, Tim Yeiser, saw even the best-funded projects drag on because of weak waterproofing, costing their owners valuable time and hundreds of thousands of dollars.

So he found a better way to waterproof concrete, without the strain and expense of building and maintaining membranes. After learning about crystalline waterproofing technology, Tim brought it to market as Dricrete. Our hydrophilic additives use water to do their own waterproofing, so structures built with Dricrete actually gain strength and integrity over time.

We put clients first at every step of the construction collaboration, whatever the scope or scale of your project.

Questions

What builders ask us.

Do I still need a membrane?

No. Dricrete removes the need for any additional waterproofing membrane, along with its installation and maintenance.

Does it hold up under hydrostatic pressure?

Yes. Dricrete can go into any concrete structure, even under hydrostatic pressure.

How do I get it on my job?

Through any ready-mix plant, either as part of their everyday non-warranty admixture program or with our 20-year guarantee option.

Does it wear out?

No. The crystals keep growing through the life of the structure, reducing porosity as they go. Concrete built with Dricrete gains strength and integrity over time.
