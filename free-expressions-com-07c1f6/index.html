About Free Expressions

We understand writers; we understand stories; and we understand the marketplace.

Free Expressions was founded in 1995 by renowned editor, author and instructor Lorin Oberweger, to support new and experienced writers with professional literary services: editing, story development, ghost writing, and internationally acclaimed workshops and webinars.

Beyond those basics, Free Expressions is about you, the writer. It's about using our decades of experience to help you reach your writing and publishing goals, and continuing to dream the dream for our clients, even in the face of a mercurial industry.

And it's about proving, over and over again, that creativity, perseverance, and belief can and will be rewarded.
