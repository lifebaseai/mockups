Questions

What HR teams ask us first.

How fast are results?

Digital checks such as PAN, Aadhaar and UAN come back in real time. Most standard background checks are complete within 24 to 72 hours.

What can you verify?

Identity, employment history, education, criminal records, address, credit, professional references and global sanctions (AML & PEP), all in one place.

Does it work with our ATS or HRMS?

Yes. VigilIQ connects with 20+ ATS and HRMS platforms, so checks start inside the recruitment workflow you already use, with no manual follow-up.

Can you check candidates who worked abroad?

Yes. We cover 170+ countries, including international employment checks, overseas criminal record searches and sanctions screening.

Will our reports hold up in an audit?

Every report is audit-ready, securely archived and structured to meet RBI, IRDAI, SEBI and NASSCOM compliance standards.

We hire in bulk. Can you keep up?

We verify 10,000+ cases every month. Bulk processing, custom workflows and the live dashboard are built for high-volume teams like staffing agencies.
