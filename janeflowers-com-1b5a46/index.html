About Jane Flowers

The sea, the land and the sky, in oils.

Paintings that make a room come to life.

Jane Flowers is an award winning Australian artist who specialises in oils on canvas: landscapes, seascapes, nautical scenes and nature.

Her deep affinity with nature and the ocean turns the beauty of the outdoors into something you live with indoors. Regular travels around the world feed the work, each painting catching the feel of one time and place.

She keeps pushing further, combining gold leaf with oils and exploring abstract themes. Her work has a strong following among art lovers, collectors and interior designers.

Away from the easel she cares about protecting the wildlife and marine life of Australia's natural ecosystems.

Questions

Good to know.

What does Jane Flowers paint?

Landscapes, seascapes, nautical scenes and nature, in oils on canvas. She also combines gold leaf with oils and explores abstract themes.

How big are the paintings?

Works on her site include canvases of 90 × 200 cm and 140 × 140 cm. Try either one in the wall planner above.

Where has her work been shown?

Her exhibition Quietitude was at Salamanca Arts Centre, Hobart, in September 2022. David and Goliath won the 2014 ANL Maritime Art Award at the Mission to Seafarers Maritime Arts Awards in Docklands, Melbourne.

Who collects her work?

Art lovers, collectors and interior designers.
