Questions

What people ask us first.

Anything else, just call the office on 01242 676767.

What happens at a valuation?

One of our property experts visits you and gives a bespoke valuation that takes into account the individuality and unique identity of your home.

Which areas do you cover?

Bishops Cleeve, Cheltenham and the surrounding villages. Our office is at 41A Church Road, Bishops Cleeve.

Is the drone photography extra?

No. Professional drone photography and video are free, as part of our property marketing. They suit every kind of home, not just large estates.

Where will my home be advertised?

On all the major property portals, including Rightmove, Zoopla and PrimeLocation, and to the buyers registered with us.

Do you value for probate, or land?

Yes. We cover residential sales, probate valuations and land appraisals.

Are you regulated?

We are members of The Property Ombudsman, fully insured and professionally regulated.
