Questions

Before you write to us

Do I have to be a writer already?

No. The workshop is nurturing for the beginning writer, and a jump-start for the burnt-out professional.

What is the workshop about?

Honoring your own voice: writing in your own rhythms, using your own language and telling your own stories. Here is where we stop the inner critic in his tracks.

What will I take home?

Your powerful one-of-a-kind golden voice, filled with self-esteem, honor and joy.

Who teaches it?

Nancy Slonim Aronie, a commentator for NPR’s All Things Considered, who won teacher of the year all three years she taught at Harvard for Robert Coles.
