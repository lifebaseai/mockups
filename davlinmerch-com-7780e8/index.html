FAQs

Not sure which sock fits? We'll point you the right way.

What is the minimum order quantity?

60 pairs per style.

How much do custom socks cost?

Premium Cotton socks are $6.95 a pair at 500 pairs. Knit beanies are $12.00 a piece and apparel starts at $11.25 a piece. Your final price comes on your quote.

How long does production take?

3–4 weeks for full production, after you approve your proof.

Will I see my design before production starts?

Yes. You get a free digital proof to approve before we make anything.

What if I don't have a design?

Send us your logo and ideas and our designers take it from there. Design packages start at $500.

What file types can I send?

AI, PDF, EPS, SVG, PNG or JPG, up to 5 MB. Our tech pack template comes as PDF or EPS.

Do you offer branded packaging?

Yes. We add UPC labels and packaging, price stickers and individual polybags, ready for the shelf.
