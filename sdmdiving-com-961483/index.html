Why we do this

We believe people protect what they love.

Our goal is not just to show you wildlife. It’s to place you in front of moments powerful enough to change the way you see the natural world forever.

Because once you truly see it… you can never fully turn away from it again.

“Our mission is to help dispel the predator myth, because people will only protect what they love.” Giving back to the animals we love is our most important work.

Before you go

Good questions, straight answers.

Anything else, ask Maritza. That’s what she’s there for.

When is the next trip?

Churchill Polar Bears, November 1–7, 2026. For our other expeditions, ask us and we’ll share what’s coming up.

Are sightings guaranteed?

No. There are no guarantees in nature. Patience and realistic expectations are part of the adventure, and Eli writes about it in “Waiting For Wildlife.”

Is it ethical to swim with whales?

It’s a question we think about a lot. Most wildlife wants nothing to do with us. What matters is the difference between forcing an encounter and being invited into one.

Who sorts out flights and hotels?

Maritza. Flights, transfers, hotels and logistics, from your first question to the final details.

Are orcas dangerous?

Many people fear them after stories of attacks and captivity. Eli has swum with orcas in Baja, Norway and New Zealand, and has written about what he learned.

Can you help me with my photography?

Yes. We offer private, one-on-one instruction in underwater photography and in finishing your images in Photoshop, tailored to your equipment, experience and goals.
