Why Paper Plane

We don’t stop until we’ve found the best solution to the puzzle: the one that fits the budget, the site, and the way the building will actually be built.

Every project is a set of decisions: budget, schedule, codes, funding, community, contractor, future growth. Our job is to make sure the important ones are the right ones.

We invest the time up front to understand the existing building, align the work with available funding, and develop construction documents thorough enough to reduce uncertainty.

When something goes wrong on site, we lead the team to a solution that protects the owner’s time and budget.
