Our story

4WT is 4word Thought.

As we move "forward" and keep making new work, we keep in mind what drives us: the four words that stand for our values. It may sound cliché, but it matters to us that everyone knows what 4WT stands for.

John Trefry founded 4WT in 2012 after leaving a venture-backed tech company, where he had built a department focused on video. He saw technology disrupting how film, TV and ads were made. So 4WT was born: high quality production, made with more efficient methods that use new technology.

Questions

Before we roll.

Can you handle the whole production?

Yes. From concept to delivery, we execute part or the entire production process. We handle large shoots, and we're happy to work with other partners.

Do you only work with big brands?

No. We work with Fortune 500s, startups and anything in between.

I have an original film or series. Is that for you?

It is. Alongside branded work we produce film, TV and stories that matter, and we'd love to hear about the content you want to share.

Can you help it reach the right people?

Yes. Whether you want to reach millions to raise brand awareness or drive conversion for a select few, distribution is part of what we do.

Can I ask for a specific director?

Yes. Request one of our writers and directors on your slate, or leave it open and we'll take it from there.
