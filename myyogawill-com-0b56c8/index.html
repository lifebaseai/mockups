Meet Rachel, founder & director

Stroke survivor. Teacher. Advocate.

“At 25, I had a massive ischemic stroke that paralyzed my left side. I was told there were many things my body would never do again. Yoga met me where I was.”

Today Rachel teaches the practice she needed back then, so no one has to walk into a studio wondering if there's room for their body. There is.

Before you come

Good questions.

Is yoga really for me?

Yes. Our classes are made for people who feel unsure if yoga is for them, including stroke survivors, trauma and neurodivergent communities. You go at your own pace.

What does it cost?

Initial consultations and sample classes are always free, and so is your first private session. Our classes and trainings are built not to break the bank, and teacher training has scholarships.

Can I bring my walker, or someone with me?

Please do. Cane, shoes, AFO, walker, chair, caregiver, emotional support friend. All welcome.

Can I join from home?

Yes. The Stroke Yoga™ Circle meets on Zoom, Wednesdays at 2:00 PM EST, for women stroke survivors. An on-demand video library is coming soon.

Should I check with my doctor first?

Yes. Yoga is not a substitute for medical care. Please consult your doctor before starting classes.
