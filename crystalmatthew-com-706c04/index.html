Crystal Matthew

I want to invest in my community, make my friends some money, and make some pretty cool art while I’m at it.

I’m a Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo Art & Design alum. After a stint in the Bay Area for a few years, I’m back home, illuminating the various touchpoints of brands with more meaning and resonance for everybody involved.

I help guide a collaborative approach to clarifying business goals and bringing vision to life. It really doesn’t have to be much more complicated than that.
