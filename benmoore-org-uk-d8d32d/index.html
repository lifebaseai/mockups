The artist

An artist first

Ben is a British artist with a degree in filmmaking and a master's in fine art. He's based in West London and is a father of three.

In 2006 he founded Art Below to turn ad space into art space, putting work by emerging artists on billboards across the London Underground.

“What would London do without eccentric lateral thinkers such as Ben Moore?”

Why the shows give back

Art that does good

Ben's brother Tom went missing in 2003. Since then Ben has worked closely with the charity Missing People and has run the London Marathon for them twice.

Many of his shows now raise money for causes like it.

  • Art WarsSignificant donations to Missing People, its official charity partner
  • 2019UnMissable, for the charity's 25th anniversary. All proceeds to Missing People
  • 2019One Small Step raised over £100K for MAG, which clears landmines
  • 2021Me, My Brain and I, for Parkinson's UK
